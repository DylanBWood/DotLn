## Release overview

Application v0.102.0 puts a bound on every role dispatch. Each dispatch kind carries a wall-clock bound and a complete-run bound in `docs/control/budgets.json`; the gate's host task, the bounded probe wrapper and the lifecycle's same-phase redispatch refuse to start past either, Claude's hooks refuse the tool calls that would start them and print the dispatch's standing at every turn, an operator authorization recorded in conversation extends the bound for that order's phase on the record, and the meter and `plan failures` report every stop, every acceptance and what followed. Until this release nothing bounded a dispatch: WO-192's executor ran 22 hours and 327 million tokens in one phase, stopped only by the operator. Operator-facing durations now read in hours and minutes.

Audience: operators running exported DotLn instances, whose budgets file gains the keys and whose sessions meet the new refusal and the acceptance route, and contributors changing the gate runner, the bounded wrapper, the lifecycle, the generated hooks, the budgets reader or the meter.

## Read before upgrading

- **New budget keys, with a policy rather than numbers.** Every role's `dispatches` entry carries `wallClockMs` and `completeRuns`; the executor also carries a `repair` object for `resume: fix`. The kit template ships `local-median` and three runs for implementation, repair, verification and final review and `null` for release close, planner and refuter. `local-median` resolves on each instance from its own completed phase history as the greater of four times the phase median and 8 h 0 min, with 8 h 0 min when no history exists and the dispatch's original start as the cutoff; no DotLn measurement is exported. A missing key reads `null` (disabled), so an export made before this release keeps `npm run meta` and `kit update` after it; an explicit number or `null` is the operator's record.
- **A sixth refusal.** Past an exhausted bound, the gate refuses a complete or review selection at its host task and records the stop on the row, the wrapper refuses a new probe, and `next` and `fix` refuse to re-dispatch the phase. Under Claude the permission hook refuses the tool call that would start a complete or review selection, a bounded probe or a worker launch, with `DOTLN_DISPATCH_BOUND`, the standing and the two routes; `operator override:` suspends that hook refusal as it does the other five, and Codex and Copilot sessions meet the in-process refusals alone. Reads, listings, focused `--only` reproductions and `npm run test:docs` stay admitted, so a stop never locks out diagnosis.
- **The acceptance route and its event.** `npm run resume -- accept-overrun`, given the metric (`wallClockMs` or `completeRuns`), `--ceiling` with a value or `unlimited`, `--reason`, the capture path with its SHA-256 digest and the actor flags, appends `DispatchBoundAccepted` in `active`, `needs-fix`, `repairing`, `verifying` or `final-review`, scoped to the order's current activation and phase and expiring when that phase completes. The capture is the operator's own words under the existing digest-checked intake contract; the current agent, including the order's executor, records the authorization with its actual attestation and keeps its writer, and no exact phrase, session exit or operator terminal is required. `unlimited` lifts a metric for the phase without resetting timing or counters. `status --json` projects `boundAcceptances` and lists the route under `legalOffRamps`. A control log that holds this event must be read by a runtime at or above this release; the control fold refuses an event type it does not know.
- **Session journals and damaged history.** Every dispatch entry binds a `boundDispatch` anchor (role, intent, order, start and phase boundary) and records `executedOrders`, so a session that executed an order keeps its own-executor exclusion when it later reviews. When any journal in the lane cannot be read, the role and its grants are still bound, the standing reads unavailable with the damaged file named, complete runs and probes are refused until the file is repaired, and `harness usage` still prints its observation and exit status beside a `dispatch standing: unavailable` line that carries the named read error. Repaired history is rescanned on the next read and the original attempt's anchor recovered, including after completion and reactivation, without rewriting a journal.
- **Durations in hours and minutes.** Gate summaries, deadline and lane-wait diagnostics, writer-refusal ages, `status`, `current.md`, the work-order index, the meter table and the budget advisories present durations rounded to the nearest minute; values under thirty seconds read `0 h 0 min`. Machine records, `--timeout` arguments and numeric flags keep their declared units.
- **Record shapes.** Gate rows carry `startedAt`, stopped rows carry `gateSelection`, and a refused row carries `boundStop` (with every exceeded metric) and `dispatchBound`; `harness usage` output gains `dispatchStanding` and `standing`; the Stop hook adds one standing line per turn; the meter snapshot and cost table carry `boundStops` and `boundAcceptances`, and `plan failures` gains a `boundStops` block with `afterStop`. Only a complete or review selection the host task admitted consumes a run; a memory or standing refusal consumes none, and rows without admission evidence keep their historical count.
- **Known issues.** A damaged gate-row store's standing error names no file ([D028](../../evidence/WO-234/decisions.md#wo-234-d028--ver-003-f7-a-damaged-gate-row-stores-standing-error-names-no-file)). An unreadable journal or gate-row store refuses complete runs with a named read error rather than naming the bound, by design ([D013](../../evidence/WO-234/decisions.md#wo-234-d013--record-the-attended-session-reading-from-the-integrated-catalog), D018). Elapsed wall clock includes attended and idle time, with no attendance clock. Past a bound, focused runs keep running until the lifecycle refuses the next redispatch. Token and dollar bounds remain unset by the operator's policy.
- **Versions.** Application v0.102.0 is a minor release over v0.101.0, assigned at activation and retimed at integration ([D011](../../evidence/WO-234/decisions.md#wo-234-d011--preserve-both-integrated-histories-and-retime-collided-components)). Compiler moves 0.32.0 to 0.33.0 for the generated six-refusal paragraph; skeleton moves 0.71.0 to 0.72.0 with harness host 0.37.0 to 0.38.0 for dispatch admission, the shared guidance and duration presentation; the console's exact pins follow; kernel 0.8.0, console 0.5.0, beacons 0.1.1 and browser-evidence 0.2.0 are unchanged, and no third-party dependency changes. Every installed role root grows by 1,418 bytes: the executor reads 32,292 within its accepted 34,953, the reviewer 31,229 within the 34,596 accepted on 2026-10-28 under the standing route, and the other roots stay within their ceilings.

## Substantive changes

**Budgets** (`scripts/lib/process-budget.mjs`, `docs/control/budgets.json`, `scripts/kit/budgets.template.json`). `readBudgets` validates `wallClockMs` and `completeRuns` for every dispatch kind and the executor's `repair` object, resolves `local-median` from the instance's completed phase attempts with an optional history cutoff, records the basis beside the resolved ceiling, and reads an absent key as `null`.

**Standing** (`scripts/lib/dispatch-bound.mjs`, `packages/skeleton/src/dispatch-journal.mjs`, `packages/skeleton/src/test-selection.mjs`, `packages/skeleton/src/duration.mjs`). `dispatchStanding` returns `within`, `exceeded` or `unset` with both metrics, the acceptances in force and the two routes; `phaseDispatchAnchor` and `requireRedispatchBound` hold a phase to its earliest unfinished dispatch; `recordBoundStop` appends each stop to the session's journal; `dispatchBoundActivity` folds stops, acceptances and the next same-order event for the meter and planning. The shared journal reader names a damaged file in a typed error, the selection classifier recognises complete and diagnostic test selections, and `formatDuration` renders hours and minutes.

**Gate and wrapper** (`scripts/test-runner.mjs`, `scripts/structural-checks.mjs`, `scripts/lib/bounded-command.mjs`). The host task reads the standing before admitting a plain or review selection, returns the bound refusal with the stop recorded on the row, and reports an unreadable standing as its own named refusal; stopped rows record their selection and start; the wrapper refuses a non-diagnostic probe at an exhausted bound before launching it.

**Lifecycle** (`scripts/resume.mjs`, `scripts/lib/control.mjs`, `scripts/lib/off-ramps.mjs`, `scripts/work-orders.mjs`). The `accept-overrun` route, its legal phases, the ceiling check against the current value and bound, the `DispatchBoundAccepted` fold and projection, the redispatch refusal in `next` and `fix`, the executed-orders exclusion for waivers, and the index, `current.md` and `status --json` projections.

**Hooks and usage** (`packages/skeleton/src/harness-host.ts`, `packages/skeleton/src/harness-command.ts`, `scripts/harness.mjs`). `bindDispatchBound` at prompt entry, CLI dispatch observation, `harness begin` and the Codex dispatch record; the sixth refusal in the permission judgment through the existing executable-invocation normalizer; the Stop hook's standing line; `harness usage` printing the standing or its named unavailability without losing the observation.

**Meter and planning** (`scripts/lib/meta.mjs`, `scripts/lib/plan-failures.mjs`). Per-order and per-dispatch `boundStops`, `boundAcceptances` and `afterStop`, retained through closed-order snapshots and recovery; the two cost-table columns; the `boundStops` block in `plan failures`, reading live journals and filed snapshots and counting unreadable snapshots.

**Roles and documents.** The shared dispatch-bound and duration sentences in every generated role root (`packages/skeleton/src/loadouts/contributor.ts`), the six-refusal paragraph in `CLAUDE.md` (`packages/compiler/src/harness.ts`), the security document's boundary paragraph with the Codex and Copilot rows, the playbook, product 07's recovery controls, preflight and planning sections, the kit README and the gate architecture record.

**Cases, controls and oracles.** New and extended cases in the harness, off-ramps, process-debt, runner, control-segment, gate-followups, gate-deadlines, work-orders, host-guard, beacon-portability, launchpad and resume suites and the new `scripts/test-dispatch-bound.mjs`; [controls.json](../../evidence/WO-234/controls.json) records the paired admission control; three role oracles pin the roots through the execution, the recovery correction and the integration with `main`; the authority, artifact-identity, verification and feedback editions are re-minted.
