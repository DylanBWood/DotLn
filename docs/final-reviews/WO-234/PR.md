# WO-234

Every role dispatch now carries a wall-clock bound and a complete-run bound read from `docs/control/budgets.json` (`wallClockMs` and `completeRuns` under each role's `dispatches` entry, with the executor's `repair` object for `resume: fix`). Implementation, repair, verification and final review select `local-median`: each consumer resolves it from its own completed phase history as the greater of four times its median and 8 h 0 min, uses 8 h 0 min without history, and cuts that history off at the dispatch's original start so a running bound cannot drift; release close, planner and refuter stay `null`, a missing key reads `null`, and the kit exports the policy, never this repository's measurements. One shared reader, `scripts/lib/dispatch-bound.mjs`, computes a dispatch's standing from the session journal's dispatch anchor (the earliest unfinished dispatch of the phase, so a new thread cannot replenish spent time or runs), the complete and review rows the host task admitted since that start, the budgets and the order's phase-scoped acceptances. The gate's host task refuses a complete or review selection past either bound and records the stop on its row with every exceeded metric; the bounded wrapper refuses a new probe; `next` and `fix` refuse to re-dispatch a phase whose earliest unfinished dispatch is exceeded; under Claude the permission hook refuses a tool call that starts a complete or review selection, a bounded probe or a worker launch with `DOTLN_DISPATCH_BOUND`, and the Stop hook prints a `dispatch standing` line at every turn with the elapsed time against its bound, the complete runs against theirs and the state, the line `node scripts/harness.mjs usage` also prints for the session. Focused `--only` runs, listings, reads and the document check stay admitted, and `operator override:` suspends the hook refusal. `npm run resume -- accept-overrun`, given the metric (`wallClockMs` or `completeRuns`), `--ceiling` with a value or `unlimited`, `--reason`, the capture path with its SHA-256 digest and the actor flags, appends `DispatchBoundAccepted`, scoped to the order's current activation and phase and expiring when that phase completes; the current agent, including the order's executor, records the operator's contextual authorization with its actual attestation and keeps its writer, and `unlimited` lifts a metric for the phase without resetting timing or counters. The meter records `boundStops`, `boundAcceptances` and `afterStop` per order and per dispatch, the cost table carries two new columns, and `plan failures` lists the block, so a planning pass reads whether a stop or an operator intervention helped. Under the operator's scope expansion of 2026-10-28 every operator-facing duration is presented in hours and minutes, rounded to the nearest minute, while machine records keep their numeric units.
