The criterion's substance is role-text isolation, which the test proves instruction by instruction after stripping that one line. D003 records the choice and makes a verifier demanding literal equality its reopening condition; VER-001 declined to demand it, and so do I.
2. **Both later trials are packages orders.** The order asks for at least one packages order and one scripts or documents order among the three. WO-110 and WO-099 are packages orders, so the scripts side rests on WO-145 itself, whose changed surfaces include `scripts/lib/meta.mjs` and `scripts/test-process-debt.mjs`. The operator chose the trials, D001 records the choice, and the planning pass that applies the reading reads D001 first. An observation for that pass, not a defect in the delivered work.
3. **The reading may be uncomputable.** D002 records `effect.wallSecondsPerOrder` and `effect.tokensPerOrder` as `null`, because one paired measurement gives a saving per eligible iteration and no frequency. The reading in D001 needs a saving over the next ten orders. If WO-110 and WO-099 also record `null`, the reading cannot be computed and the support stays optional by default. That is the honest state, and D001 and D002 both carry reopening conditions that reach it.
4. **A decline that ran nothing cannot be recorded.** `checkExperiment` requires non-empty `cost.commands` and `effect.commands` even for `execution: "declined"`. This follows the order's design, which asks every record for the commands that show both; an executor who declines after any investigation can name what they ran. Carried to the pass that revisits the contract.
5. **The bootstrap trial was manual.** The executor states that trial one enacted the support's instructions before the support existed. Criterion 3 asks for the record, which exists; behavior arising from equipment alone is first observable in the later trials.
6. **The adjacent repair is inside its authority.** D004 removed "live model verification" from the README's not-yet sentence and reworded the everyday outline's item 11, because WO-056 landed that bounded evidence in the same release block.
