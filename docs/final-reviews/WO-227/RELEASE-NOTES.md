## Release overview

Application v0.91.0 makes an instance's outward publication read as the operator intended. The fork's first target draft on 2026-10-21 was refused as `vocabulary.launchpad (line 147)`, a generated Change-table row naming a fixture whose file name carried the ordinary word "launchpad", and the operator's correction of the same day said the lint was for launchpad identifiers and for DotLn-specific usage judged in context, never a ban on English words. A second correction said a private term that is also an ordinary word is refused as the system's name, not as the word. This release turns the public word ban into a review the reviewer confirms, gives each private term a mode, adds the identifier rule the correction names, admits an empty list in either file, judges the generated Change table apart from the reviewed prose, warns at planning when an order names a file a review word would flag, gives the stored pull-request body a shape, and lets a target order carry its reviewed notes on its Release, which the fork's operator had called unreadable in its thin form.

Audience: operators and reviewers of exported instances that publish into a target repository, above all one whose private list has unmarked terms today; operators of this repository who write pull-request bodies and run release preparation; and the planner who reads the boarded follow-ups.

## Read before upgrading

- **Words are reviewed, not banned.** The vocabulary file's key is `reviewTerms`, an array of words that may be empty; `terms`, the key WO-063 wrote, is read as the same list for one kit release with the advisory `outward-vocabulary: terms is read as reviewTerms for this kit release; rename the key`, and a file carrying both keys refuses. A listed word in a target's branch, commit, title or composed body is a `vocabulary.review` item that names the word, its line and the part it sits in; it refuses nothing on its own. The publish refuses until the reviewer passes `--confirm-terms` with the composed body's `sha256:` digest, the one the refusal printed, on `worktree publish WO-NNN --target-worktree`, its `--draft` form, `worktree publish WO-NNN --target` or `release close WO-NNN`; a stale digest refuses naming the composed body's digest, and a malformed value refuses before any effect. The confirmation, its digest, match count and actor attestation (harness, selected effort, source and role as the session reports them; model `unknown` because no harness reads one back), is recorded on the publication event and as a `ReviewTermsConfirmed` row in the session's harness journal when one is observable ([D004](../../evidence/WO-227/decisions.md#wo-227-d004), [D014](../../evidence/WO-227/decisions.md#wo-227-d014)).
- **Each private term has a mode, and unmarked terms change meaning.** A line of `docs/control/local/terms.txt` is `[refuse: | review: ]term`. A `refuse:` term refuses every outward artifact that carries it; an unmarked or `review:` term is a review item of a target publication, confirmed the same way. Only the two mode words before a colon are markers, so a term holding a colon stays a term; a term listed twice keeps the stricter mode; a marker with no term refuses naming its line. A present list with no terms is `present` with a count of zero and refuses nothing; an absent list is `unavailable` and still refuses publication. In committed text (`npm run terms -- check`, the kit export, planning receipts and the follow-up register) every match of either mode still refuses. An operator whose list has unmarked terms today should mark `refuse:` the ones that must keep refusing before the first publish under this release; the review-by-default reading is the operator's correction, and [planning receipt 052](../../planning/refutations/2026-10-22-planning-5bb6c2ac729c3e47-052.md) records the standing known issue that a reviewer, not the operator, confirms such a match, with its reopening condition.
- **Launchpad identifiers never leave the launchpad.** `WO-NNN`, `WO-NNN-DNNN`, `FUP-<16 hex>`, `VER-NNN`, `FINAL-NNN`, `REVIEW-NNN` and `WS-NNN` refuse as whole tokens, on the written and the rendered view, in a target's branch, commits, pull-request title and body, tag message and Release body under `identifier.launchpad`, naming the identifier, the line and the part; a hyphen or path separator does not end a token, so `docs/WO-006-notes.md` and `fix/handle-wo-006` carry the identifier. The kit's fixed records commit message, a `Closes #N` reference and a launchpad artifact's own `# WO-NNN` title pass. `final-review-result pass` for a target order refuses a reviewed commit carrying an identifier before recording, because a commit cannot be repaired after the review without a new commit. The forms are the launchpad's own; an instance that mints other shapes lists them in `reviewTerms`.
- **Generated text is judged as generated text.** The composed body is the reviewed prose, the generated Change table and, on the reviewed publish, the target-checks section, each a named part; a Change row names its added path, a row of a path the target's base already held is not reported, and a kit record's path is exempt from the identifier rule only. An exemption covers only a match lying wholly inside its part or row: a listed term running from a base-held row into an added path, from a written record link into the author's fragment, or past a display name is still reported ([D033](../../evidence/WO-227/decisions.md#wo-227-d033--lift-a-rule-only-from-a-match-lying-wholly-in-an-exempting-part-or-row)). Private matches keep their complete line range through the redacted observation, and every row in that range keeps its path and other matched text redacted in diagnostics ([D035](../../evidence/WO-227/decisions.md#wo-227-d035--carry-the-complete-private-line-range-through-exemptions-and-redaction)). A private match joining two base-held rows is reported, conservatively. On the draft route the prepared commit is recorded before the body is judged, so the digest a refusal printed is the digest of the body that publishes.
- **Planning warns early.** `npm run plan -- check` lists, as an advisory, each backticked path in an open target order's Execution plan that carries a review word or a launchpad identifier, with the remedy (rename the file, or list the word deliberately); a launchpad order's paths are its own vocabulary and an invalid vocabulary file is itself an advisory. Nothing refuses.
- **The stored pull-request body has a shape.** Its first non-blank line is the level-one heading, a prose paragraph (a list-item or quoted paragraph counts; comments, code blocks, headings, tables, definitions and images alone do not) stands before the process-meter block, and that block is the last block. `release prepare` and the regenerated body lift an existing block from wherever it stood and append it after the last prose paragraph, changing no other byte of an LF body; `worktree publish` and `check-surfaces` refuse the current order's stored body that breaks the shape, the published body admits the product-gate block after the meter, and no historical body is judged. An instance's stored publication body must therefore open with `# WO-NNN`. A CRLF body's line endings change around the lifted block, boarded as [D028](../../evidence/WO-227/decisions.md#wo-227-d028--board-the-crlf-byte-change-of-the-meter-relocation).
- **A target Release can carry the reviewed notes.** An order header `**Target release notes:** reviewed` makes the Release body the five sections of the order's `RELEASE-NOTES.md` at the merged revision, the order title once under the overview, links to records the kit moved into the target written absolute to the target at the tagged commit, every other relative link as its text, and the close line last; `worktree publish` for such an order requires and parses the notes beside the body, and `release close` refuses before any tag push when the file is absent or invalid, naming it. `objective`, or no header, keeps the title, objective and close line byte for byte. The tag message and the body are linted as a target's `release-body`: identifiers refuse outside the kit's frame (the order title, the close line and the address, tagged commit and record path the renderer itself writes for a moved record), the registered `release.displayName` reads as a name for the public words, review items wait for `--confirm-terms` on the close and `--dry-run` prints them with the digest without refusing. Only the bytes the renderer writes for a moved record are its frame: a target URL the author writes, at any commit, and the query or fragment of a record link are judged like the rest of the notes, though a fragment may name the closing order's own identifiers ([D031](../../evidence/WO-227/decisions.md#wo-227-d031--exempt-only-the-bytes-the-renderer-writes-for-a-moved-record)). The body's source, digest, review and confirmation are bound in the close record: an attempt that pushed the tag and still owes the Release republishes the bytes it bound or refuses on changed notes, a completed Release keeps its recorded binding across every recheck, and a Release published with the legacy body before the order named the reviewed notes reports `existing`.
- **Versions.** Application v0.91.0 is a minor release over v0.90.0, assigned at activation under the order's classification. No component version changes: compiler 0.30.0, skeleton 0.65.0, console 0.4.5, kernel 0.6.0, beacons 0.1.1 and browser-evidence 0.1.0 stay, the lockfile is unchanged and no dependency is added. No role text changed.

## Substantive changes

**The lint** (`scripts/lib/outward-lint.mjs`, `scripts/lib/terms.mjs`, `scripts/outward-lint.mjs`). `validateOutwardVocabulary` returns the normalized vocabulary with its advisories and admits an empty review list; `launchpadIdentifierForms` and the `identifier.launchpad` rule run on target artifacts over the written and rendered views; the term loop yields `vocabulary.review` findings and a `review` status with the match count; `release-body` is a new kind; `sections` label each finding with the narrowest part holding its start and lift a rule only when every byte of the span lies in exempting parts; `exempt` spans drop the review words a display name holds; `matchReviewTerms` and `matchLaunchpadIdentifiers` are shared matchers. `parseLocalTerms` reads the per-term mode, and `checkLocalTerms` returns `{ status: "present", terms }`, keying each match by starting line, ending line and mode in its redacted diagnostic. The outward adapter forwards the range and mode, exposes `readOutwardVocabulary`, takes `exemptText`, and the CLI gains `--target` and exit code 4 for a review result.

**The publish** (`scripts/lib/target-publish.mjs`, `scripts/lib/github-body.mjs`, `scripts/lib/confirm-terms.mjs`, `scripts/worktree.mjs`, `scripts/lib/target-commit-lint.mjs`, `scripts/lib/target-records.mjs`, `scripts/lib/off-ramps.mjs`). `composeTargetBody` builds the body from labelled parts and returns each Change row's offsets, path, base-held and kit flags; `baseTreePaths` reads the target's base tree for the changed paths only; `lintArtifacts` judges every artifact as a target's, lifts row exemptions only over a row's own bytes, redacts every diagnostic overlapping a private span, prints the vocabulary advisories before any refusal, and returns the confirmed review or, in preview, the pending items and digest; `reviewConfirmation` records the confirmation with the session's attestation and appends the journal row; the three publish routes and `publishBoundTargetCommit` carry the confirmation into the publication event; the draft route records its prepared commit before judging the body. One shared parser, `takeConfirmTerms`, reads `--confirm-terms` for `worktree publish` and `release close`. `lintReviewedTargetCommits` refuses an identifier in a reviewed target commit, and the kit's records commit message is linted as a target's.

**The release** (`scripts/release.mjs`, `scripts/lib/release-notes.mjs`, `scripts/lib/github-repository.mjs`, `scripts/lib/github-body.mjs`). `targetReleaseNotesSource` reads the header; `targetReleaseNotesBody` renders the five sections with `absoluteBodyLinks`' new `map` and `written` options, which map a resolved path onto the moved-record tree and report the offsets of each destination the rewrite wrote; `prepareTargetRelease` lints the tag message and body, binds `publication.target.body` (source, digest, review, confirmation), refuses changed bytes after a tag-pushing attempt and retains a completed Release's binding; `ensureRelease` admits earlier bodies and body digests and reports the published bytes through `bindBody`; the close summary prints the body's source and review state. `withMeterBlockLast` and `METER_MARKERS` own the meter's placement for `release prepare` and `regeneratedPullRequestBody`; `pullRequestBodyShapeFailures` and `assertPullRequestBodyShape` judge the three structural rules, called by `worktree publish` for the current order's body and by `githubBodyRule` in `check-surfaces`.

**Planning** (`scripts/lib/plan-receipts.mjs`, `scripts/lib/entropy-review.mjs`). `targetOrderPathAdvisories` extracts an open target order's Execution-plan paths and matches them against the review list and the identifier forms; `checkPlanGate` returns them as `advisories`. Plan and entropy receipts record the private list's presence alone, never its count.

**Tests.** The outward-lint suite covers the seven identifier forms across five kinds with their launchpad controls, the kit texts, compatibility spellings and rendered markup, the review-word status with sections and exempt spans, the vocabulary file's three shapes, the private modes and malformed markers, distinct complete line ranges with invalid endpoints, and the CLI's `--target` flag and exit codes. The github-body suite covers written-destination offsets, the composed body's parts and rows, the shape rules over non-prose blocks and three line endings, the meter relocation and the link map. The target-publish suite covers kit paths, a review word crossing a base-held row, private matches over two and three rows in all three spellings, and the loadout route's confirmation and recorded attestation. The target-worktree suites cover the draft and reviewed publishes with their refusals before any forge effect, the three-row private case, and the Release's legacy bytes, reviewed rendering, pre-push refusals, bound digest, retries, rechecks and provenance boundaries. The plan-refutation suite covers the planning advisory and the redacted diagnostic's new shape; the release shell suite covers `release prepare`'s relocation and `check-surfaces`'s shape judgment; the harness, launchpad export and planning, process-debt, release composition, release fixtures and runner suites follow the changed shapes.
