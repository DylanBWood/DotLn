# WO-249 — Criteria judge the product: a verifier records a criterion that no candidate can meet as defective by class and the order goes on without a waiver, a waiver is the operator's words asking for it and can be retracted, a handoff the operator widened can be reopened, a failing verification names the criterion or the regression it failed on, and planning lints criteria for the five defect classes (v0.110.0)

Until now an order whose acceptance criterion no candidate could meet, because of how it was written, stopped until the operator typed a waiver: five waivers on four core orders and two on the fork's, none a failure of the product, each an interruption at the laptop. A waiver once recorded could not be undone except by editing the control segment by hand, a handoff the operator widened kept its stale ledger until a verification failed, a verifier could announce a block on an input it constructed, and the briefing offered a waiver route to a session that then took it from words that asked for none.

Now the order goes on. In `verifying` or `final-review` the verifier or the reviewer, never the order's executor, records `npm run resume -- defect <criterion> --class <class> --evidence <text>` in one of five closed classes (bookkeeping, later-phase, absent-input, host-behavior, contradiction); the event carries the SHA-256 of the criterion's text, so an amended criterion takes a fresh record and the same text keeps the one it has. A report judges the criterion on `**Criterion <id>:** unmet, defective (<class>) by <ordinal>` with the judge's own evidence in its paragraph; the line is refused when the record does not stand at that ordinal, its class differs, a retraction undid it, its paragraph is empty or the criterion's text no longer hashes to it, and a pass must judge every standing record, on that line or a `met` line, which stands over it. The final review confirms the record on the same line or, finding the criterion judgeable, fails with a blocking finding whose basis names it. `release prepare` writes one line per defective or waived criterion above the stored pull-request body's process table, naming the `retract` command, so the operator who merges by hand reads the record first; `final-review-result pass` refuses a stored body whose lines are stale and `worktree publish` one changed after the pass.

The operator's words now reach the record in both directions. `retract <ordinal>` undoes a waiver or a defect record from the operator's captured words, legal from `verifying` through `closed`: before the merge it returns a closed order to `needs-fix` with the retraction as its failure source, and release close refuses while the retraction is newer than the latest passing final review on `origin/main`, read from the merged record and from the order's registered worktree; after the merge it is recorded from main's pending lane and changes no phase. `reopen-handoff` returns an order the operator widened after its handoff from `ready-to-verify` to the phase the handoff left, clearing the unmet criteria, so the next completion writes a fresh ledger. `waive` records only from the operator's words asking for that criterion's waiver, and the unmet briefing prints the three routes with that rule. A fail names what it failed on: `verification-result fail` and `final-review-result fail` refuse a report whose findings block is absent or unreadable, and one whose criterion lines are all met, waived or defective while the block lists no blocking entry; a blocking entry carries a `basis` naming the criterion or the behavior `main` had and a `reproduction` path under the order's evidence directory, advised when missing. A heading inside a fenced example no longer ends a judgment's paragraph. `plan failures` counts the three events and each defect by class, `index --check` prints an advisory for every open order's criterion whose wording often marks one of the five classes and refuses nothing, and every role's text states the rules.
