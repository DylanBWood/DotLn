## Release overview

An order no longer waits at the laptop for a criterion that no candidate can meet because of how it was written. The verifier or the final reviewer records it defective in one of five closed classes (bookkeeping, later-phase, absent-input, host-behavior, contradiction) with evidence, the next independent role confirms it on the same report line or disputes it with a blocking finding, the pull-request body names the record above its process table for the operator who merges by hand, and the order goes on. A waiver is recorded only from the operator's words asking for it, and the operator's words can now undo a waiver or a defect record (`retract`) and reopen a handoff the operator widened (`reopen-handoff`). A failed verification or final review names what it failed on, and planning's index check prints an advisory for every open criterion whose wording often marks one of the five classes. This release is for instance operators, verifiers, reviewers and planners; the operator's ordinary phrases are unchanged.

## Read before upgrading

- `verification-result fail` and `final-review-result fail` now refuse a report whose findings block is absent or unreadable, naming the block, and one whose criterion lines are all met, waived or defective while the block lists no `blocking` entry. A failing report carries one findings block between its `dotln-findings:start` and `dotln-findings:end` marker lines, `[]` when the unmet line is the basis, and a blocking entry carries `"basis": {"criterion": "N"}` or `{"regression": "the behavior main had"}` and a `"reproduction"` path under the order's evidence directory; an entry without them prints one advisory and records. An instance fixture that records a fail from a bare report is refused until it adds the block.
- `npm run resume -- defect N --class CLASS --evidence TEXT` with the actor flags is legal in `verifying` and `final-review` for the verifier or the reviewer; the order's executor is refused, as it is for `waive`. A report line `**Criterion N:** unmet, defective (CLASS) by ORDINAL` must state the judge's own evidence in its paragraph; a pass must judge every standing record of a criterion the order still declares, and a `met` line stands over the record. The record binds the SHA-256 of the criterion's text as the order's numbered list declares it: an amended criterion refuses the old line and takes a fresh record.
- `npm run resume -- retract ORDINAL --reason TEXT --capture PATH --capture-hash sha256:DIGEST` with the actor flags undoes a `CriterionWaived` or `CriterionDefectRecorded` record of the current activation from `verifying`, `verified`, `needs-fix`, `final-review` or `closed`. In `closed` it reads the pull request's merge state after fetching `origin/main`: unmerged, it runs in the order's worktree and returns the order to `needs-fix`, and release close refuses while the retraction is newer than the latest passing final review on `origin/main`, read from the merged record and from the order's registered worktree; merged, it runs from main, waits in the pending lane for the next `worktree start` and changes no phase; a released order takes none. A fetch that fails refuses, because an unseen merge cannot be told from an open pull request.
- `npm run resume -- reopen-handoff --reason TEXT --capture PATH --capture-hash sha256:DIGEST` with the actor flags is legal in `ready-to-verify` only, returns the order to `active` or `repairing`, clears the recorded unmet criteria, and times the reopened attempt from the reopening.
- `status --json` adds `defectiveCriteria`, `retractedOffRamps` and `reopenedHandoffs`, and `legalOffRamps` lists `defect`, `retract` and `reopen-handoff` where they are legal; `current.md` and the work-order index carry the same lines. A consumer that pins the exact status shape updates its pin.
- For a launchpad order in a repository with `docs/control/budgets.json`, `release prepare` writes one line per waived or defective criterion between the `dotln-criterion-records:start` and `dotln-criterion-records:end` marker lines directly above the stored pull-request body's process-meter block, each naming its `retract` command; `final-review-result pass` refuses a stored body whose lines differ from the record while preparation can still rewrite them, and `worktree publish` refuses one changed after the pass. A target order's body and an unmetered repository's body carry none, and a body that names lines where none apply is refused.
- `npm run work-orders -- index --check`, and through it `plan check` and `npm run test:docs`, print `criteria advisory: WO-NNN criterion N:` followed by what matched for each active or open order's criterion that names a figure with a unit or a dollar amount, a median, ceiling, budget or cap beside a figure, `HEAD`, `git log`, a `..` or `...` revision range, or the phrases "after the review gate", "at release close" and "after the final review". The screen refuses nothing; a figure can rightly name a record.
- `plan failures` counts `CriterionDefectRecorded`, `OffRampRetracted` and `HandoffReopened` as off-ramps and each defect by class in `defectClasses`, leaving out a record the operator retracted; the `plan start` block carries the off-ramp counts by kind without the class breakdown. A drive stops at a retraction or a reopened handoff, as at a waiver, and goes on past a defect record; the meter counts the two operator acts as directions.
- A heading inside a fenced example no longer ends a judgment's paragraph in `criterionJudgments`, so a receipt cited after the example belongs to the judgment above it; a findings block ends the paragraph, so it is never read as a defective line's evidence.
- Component versions: `@dotln/skeleton` 0.78.0, retimed above the 0.77.0 that WO-251's release took, with the console's pin and both lockfile entries; the harness-host compatibility pin stays at 0.41.0 and `@dotln/compiler` at 0.36.0. No external dependency changes. The installed role roots grow by this order's sentences: on the integrated tree the verifier measures 35,447 bytes against its effective 35,221 and the planner 26,449 against 24,576; both overruns are boarded for optimization with equivalent usefulness, and a dated planner acceptance the executor recorded under the retired route was withdrawn by the review (D025).

## Substantive changes

**The lifecycle.** `scripts/lib/control.mjs` validates and folds `CriterionDefectRecorded` (criterion id, text hash, class, evidence, actor), `OffRampRetracted` (the retracted record's ordinal, type and criterion, the operator's capture, and in `closed` the pull request's merge state) and `HandoffReopened` (the reopened handoff's ordinal and type, the operator's capture); the fold refuses a retraction of a record that no longer stands or a merge state named outside `closed`, and a reopening of any handoff but the one the order waits on. The state projects `defectiveCriteria`, `retractedOffRamps` and `reopenedHandoffs`, returns a closed order to `needs-fix` on an unmerged retraction with `retraction-ORDINAL` as its failure source, and returns a reopened order to the phase its handoff left with its unmet criteria cleared. `unrepairedRetraction` is the one rule release close reads for the merged record and for the order's worktree.

**The commands.** `scripts/resume.mjs` adds `defect`, `retract` and `reopen-handoff` beside `waive`, with the executor refusal shared by `waive`, `defect` and `retract` once the phase admits the command and its arguments parse. `defect` refuses a class outside the five, a criterion the order does not declare, a waived criterion, a second record for the same text and a record of text whose record the operator retracted; it replaces the record of an amended criterion. `retract` refuses another event kind, an earlier activation's record and one already retracted or replaced; in `closed` it reads the merge as release close does, names the order's worktree from main for an unmerged pull request, records from main into the pending lane for a merged one, and refuses a released order. `reopen-handoff` reopens the latest `ImplementationReady` or `RepairCompleted`. The repair briefing names the retraction's capture as the failure source, the unmet briefing prints the defect route, the words rule and the amendment route, and each record's message says when the stored pull-request body's lines are stale.

**The report judgment.** `scripts/lib/off-ramps.mjs` reads the defective line form, judges it against the record's ordinal, class and the criterion's current text hash, requires evidence outside the report's header fields on the line or a detailed line for the same criterion, admits a `met` line over a standing record, admits a fail's plain `unmet` line only when a blocking finding's basis names the criterion, requires a pass to judge every standing record of a declared criterion, and refuses a fail without a readable findings block or without an unmet line or a blocking entry. `scripts/lib/review-findings.mjs` separates the block reader from the count, reads a blocking entry's basis through one rule, and advises on a missing basis, a basis naming an undeclared or met criterion, or a missing or non-file reproduction. `criterionJudgments` ends a paragraph only at an unfenced heading, the next judgment or the findings block.

**Publication and release close.** `scripts/lib/github-body.mjs` renders the criterion-record lines, lifts a stale block, keeps them out of the prose-before-meter test and judges a stored body against the record through one rule shared by `release prepare`, the regenerated body, `final-review-result pass` and `worktree publish`; `scripts/release.mjs` writes the lines at preparation and refuses a close whose order holds a retraction newer than its latest passing final review on `origin/main`, in the merged record or in the order's registered worktree. `scripts/lib/pending-overrides.mjs` admits a merged-form retraction into main's pending lane, refuses a duplicate there, refuses one a worktree start already carried or the order's worktree recorded before a merge that left it unrepaired, and never folds a carried record twice.

**Planning, the drive and the meter.** `scripts/lib/plan-failures.mjs` counts the three events and each defect by class and keeps the class breakdown off the `plan start` block; `scripts/work-orders.mjs` adds the criteria screen to `index --check` and renders the three record kinds on each order's card; `scripts/lib/plan-receipts.mjs` returns the advisories as `criteriaAdvisories` from `plan check`; `scripts/test-runner.mjs` relays the advisory lines from a passing document task; `scripts/lib/drive-route.mjs` stops at a retraction or a reopened handoff, reads a closed order's merge through `orderMerged`, and no longer lets an earlier cycle's failing report speak for a repair a retraction opened; `scripts/lib/meta.mjs` counts the two operator acts as directions and `scripts/lib/control-time.mjs` times a reopened attempt from its reopening.

**The roles and the documents.** `packages/skeleton/src/loadouts/contributor.ts` carries the off-ramps sentence with the three commands and the words rule for every non-router role, the executor's five-class sentence, the verifier's funnel and findings-block sentence, the reviewer's defective line forms and basis, and the planner's five-class screen and lint; `npm run sync` regenerated both skill roots, and the role oracles `wo249-role-baseline.json` and `wo249-integration-role-baseline.json` pin them. Product 07 §Operator resume phrases, §Operator recovery controls, §Operator-opened planning pass, §Verification review and attack and §Discipline, product 04, product 08 §PRs and commits and `docs/KIT.md` state the commands, the classes, the words rule, the fail rule and the lint as built.

**Tests.** `scripts/test-off-ramps.mjs` adds the WO-120 to WO-124 fixtures: every route's legal phases, the five classes through the CLI, the executor refusal, the hash, class, ordinal and empty-paragraph refusals, header fields as non-evidence, the retraction in open phases and across activations, the reopened handoff to `active` and `repairing`, the fail's basis and advisories, one record per criterion, a struck criterion's standing record, the fenced heading, and a closed order's retraction before and after the merge in its own repository with a main checkout, a worktree and a bare origin, through the pending lane and its carry; `scripts/test-release.sh` adds `criterion_records`, `unrepaired_retraction` and `worktree_retraction`; `scripts/test-github-body.mjs`, `scripts/test-work-orders.mjs`, `scripts/test-plan-refutation.mjs`, `scripts/test-drive.mjs` and `scripts/test-process-debt.mjs` add their WO-249 cases, and the fixtures that record a fail now carry a findings block.
