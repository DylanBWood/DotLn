## Release overview

WO-109 is a documentation and research order with an honest no-release close; its work rides the next tag above `v0.14.0`. It adds the Re-mining Well, a documented practice for re-reading original sources against what survived into the product, and its first bounded pilot draw. The draw read twenty root stones (twelve raw payloads and eight ledger and product descendants) through four lenses, measured a strict local yield of zero against a threshold of two, and therefore recommends no identical second draw. It preserves a ten-card atlas, 166 research records from operator-authorized public book and interaction research, and a discoverable concept-registry proposal for planning. Audience: the operator and planning sessions choosing the next application work. Nothing changes for anyone running the packages.
