## Release overview

DotLn's execution guide is the document every role loads at cold start, and it had reached 2,016 lines by collecting harness observations alongside the lifecycle contract. This release moves those observations out. The Codex sandbox-approval procedure and the whole of §Model-specific notes now live in `docs/AI-HARNESS-SECURITY.md` and the playbook, each paragraph in exactly one home, each with a dated pointer where it left, and the guide keeps the executor's operating contract. A relocation table in the order's evidence accounts for every paragraph — relocated, retired or kept — and a duplicated procedure that all six generated role skills already carried is retired in favor of a pointer. The guide is 83 lines and 6,245 bytes smaller, and every measured role's directed read is 252 bytes shorter. This release is for anyone who reads or maintains the guide: the harness rules are still all there, each in one findable place, and the contract is no longer interleaved with per-CLI observations.

## Read before upgrading

**No rule was dropped, and nothing executable changed.** No package, script, test, hook, generated skill, fixture or dependency changed in this release. Every relocated rule was checked sentence by sentence against its named home before the paragraph left, and the retired ones were checked against the generated role skills that carry them.

**Anchors and cited sections still resolve.** `## Model-specific notes` keeps its heading as a pointer stub, because sixty work orders, four Cites blocks, five in-repo links and the publication index all name it; dropping it would have broken cited-section resolution.

**The measured saving is small, and it is not the relocation.** The relocated sections were never in any role's directed read set, so moving them changed no total. The 252-byte reduction per role comes entirely from retiring the duplicated goal-alignment procedure. That is 0.7% to 1.1% of these totals, and no latency, token or dollar saving is inferred from it.

**The guide no longer lists the six equipped roles by name.** The retired sentence named executor/fixer, verifier, reviewer, planner, refuter and release-close. The fact is unchanged and verifiable in all twelve generated skill files, but a reader of the guide alone will not see the enumeration.

**Measured totals come from a fixture, not from your order.** The method resolves its task selectors through the synthetic WO-999 fixture. A real order's cold start also pulls the sections its own Cites block names, so these totals are not an estimate of every session; the guide's §Read order paragraph now says so.

## Substantive changes

**The guide keeps the operating contract.** §Operator resume phrases step 2 replaces the dated Codex sandbox-approval paragraph with a pointer to the playbook's harness safety baseline, which carries the first-invocation approval rule, and to the security document's explanation of the checkpoint warning, tested versions, verification and rollback. §Model-specific notes becomes a stub holding the behavioral-guidance paragraph, one sentence restating the attestation contract, and three pointers. §Read order for a cold start gains a paragraph naming the new homes and the measured fact.

**One home for harness readback.** `docs/AI-HARNESS-SECURITY.md` gains `## Harness version, model and effort readback`, holding the WO-132 attestation-field and effort observations, the `ultra` and `ultra code` normalization, the Codex thread-readback and `--account-label` grammar, and the WO-126 version-line attestation with its session-detection channels and decision links, all with relative links rebased to the `docs/` level. Its Copilot section gains `### Selected-session readback, completion and counters` for the three WO-146 paragraphs — bare entry and selected-session readback, the ordinary completion line, and the counter and AI-credit scopes.

**Control-time detail joins the timing paragraph it belongs to.** The playbook's §Resume command surface gains the WO-028 sentences it did not already carry: `recordedAt` at append and its schema-1 optionality, that timing orders no event and grants no legal action, the dated discovery observation with its 120 committer times and 15 unknowns, the public-profile statement, and the separate WO-126 usage channel that preserves source, scope and unavailable values and never a fabricated price. `briefing` joins `status` and `times` in the read-only command list.

**A duplicated procedure retired, with its unique clauses kept.** The goal card's **All phases** introduction restated the Goal Alignment and Process Cost duties that all six generated role skills carry in both skill roots. It is now a dated pointer. Its evidence-of-benefit, intervention-risk, verification-and-handoff and legal/release-authority clauses are retained in the same section beside the unchanged NoOp paragraph; the eight-lens table, mission, critical path, accuracy rule, platform lens and decision surfaces are untouched.
