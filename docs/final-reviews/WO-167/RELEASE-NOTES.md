## Release overview

The execution guide, the document every DotLn role reads at cold start, now states each rule once, in the sentence where it applies, with a bracketed citation to the decision or planning document that holds its date and rationale. Its 33 dated amendment paragraphs are folded into the sentences they amended, its nine planning candidates now live in the planning map, and the guide falls from 188,390 to 154,129 bytes (2,617 to 2,068 lines). Its ceiling falls with it, from 188,399 to 157,212 bytes, so the guide was 9 bytes from a standstill and now has 3,083 bytes of room for the queued orders that write into it. The release is for anyone who reads or edits the guide; the fold changed no rule's meaning.
