# WO-167

The execution guide is the document every DotLn role reads at cold start, and it only ever grew: amendments were added as dated paragraphs beside the sentences they changed, so a reader met a rule, then its correction, and had to reconcile the two, and planning candidates sat between the operating sections. At `5f3849ec` it held 188,390 of its 188,399 allowed bytes, so no order could add a sentence to it. This pull request lands WO-167: the guide states each rule once, in place, with a citation to where its rationale lives, and its ceiling is lowered to hold it there.

- **Every dated paragraph is folded into the sentence it amends.** 33 standalone dated paragraphs and the eleven dated leads of the Discipline list become the current rule plus a bracketed citation that keeps the date and names the decision or planning document holding the rationale, for example `[WO-041 breakout, 2026-09-07]`. Where a paragraph recorded history rather than a rule (the consumption records, the WO-053 amendment example, the expired interim instruction before WO-158), it becomes one citation line. The [fold table](../../evidence/WO-167/fold-table.md) gives each paragraph, its carrying sentence and the citation ([D001 to D003](../../evidence/WO-167/decisions.md)).
- **The nine candidates move to the planning map.** They sit under §Moved from the execution guide (2026-09-28) as level-three headings with their original slugs, so `work-order-map.md#candidate--…` links resolve; their dated leads become status words with citations and their links are rebased. The one live rule a candidate held, the operator's platform-first standard that the planning refuter reads, stays in product 07 with the bytes the refuter extracts unchanged ([D004](../../evidence/WO-167/decisions.md#wo-167-d004)). The register records each moved candidate as a duplicate of its map row with its history kept, through nine `followups --apply` requests ([D005](../../evidence/WO-167/decisions.md#wo-167-d005)).
- **The guide is 34,261 bytes smaller and stays that way.** Product 07 goes from 188,390 bytes and 2,617 lines to 154,129 and 2,068; its ceiling goes from 188,399 to 157,212, the landing count plus two per cent that every other product document follows, and its docs-check baseline is emptied, so a new dated paragraph or candidate heading is refused ([D007](../../evidence/WO-167/decisions.md#wo-167-d007)). The map grows from 381,012 to 412,672 bytes, outside any role's directed reads.
- **The heading check the order relies on can now fail.** `node scripts/harness-context.mjs --check` measured bytes only and exited 0 with a cited heading renamed, which failed [VER-001](../../verifications/WO-167/VER-001.md). It now resolves every document selector in `CLAUDE.md` and the twelve installed role skills with the strict resolver and exits 1 naming each unresolved section or file; budget advisories still never refuse and its measurement output is unchanged. A regression in `scripts/test-process-debt.mjs` renames a cited heading and removes a floor-read skill ([D011](../../evidence/WO-167/decisions.md#wo-167-d011)).
- **Integrating main met two defects, both fixed with regressions.** `worktree integrate --continue` refused because the order's uncommitted control log was inside the integration's own stash; it now judges the phase and order path its receipt recorded. The planning gate refused the retimed title because a judged release label had no admitted update; one label replacement is now reported as `release-retiming` and any other title or body change still refuses ([D013](../../evidence/WO-167/decisions.md#wo-167-d013)).
