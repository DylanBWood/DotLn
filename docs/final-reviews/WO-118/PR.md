# WO-118

An exported DotLn starter now carries one filed intent through the whole resident-owned loop to a resolved pull request with nobody tending it, and this release ships the runtime repairs that made that possible. In the witnessed run, the resident of a fresh starter instance took a filed intent through all fourteen phases while presence was away, from the source bundle and contract through a governed source change, independent verification and delivery to a review loop that accepted one automated suggestion and rejected a planted wrong one with evidence. On the way it replaced a native writer killed mid-episode without a second commit and resumed the same admitted intent after the resident itself was stopped and relaunched. The instance's own console showed the work live, and `dotln audit` reproduces it afterwards. This is the product exit on the critical path; WO-083, which depends on this run, takes the same loop to an operator's own repository.

**What the run showed.** One witnessed run from a fresh export of the reviewed subject carries criteria 1 to 5 together ([evidence](../../evidence/WO-118/README.md), [receipt](../../evidence/WO-118/witnessed-run/receipt.json)). Before the terminal state the only human-origin events are the presence-away setup event and the two intent filings; the fault injections and control inputs are inventoried with their timing. The writer was killed with SIGKILL after one heartbeat and replaced under the same command and episode once its process group had stopped. The resident's SIGTERM and relaunch fell between the bundle and contract steps, with one admission and one opened vertical throughout. Delivery opened a pull request with a generated title and body. Triage accepted the delayed whitespace suggestion, which one repair commit fixed and verification confirmed at the delivered head, and rejected the planted instruction with three evidence references; both threads are resolved. The console painted 73 frames: every step lasting two seconds or more appeared as live work beside the ambiguous control's hold, and the finished continuation left the actor list. All eight representative parity items are observed-met. Efficiency, tokens, cost and human time are unknown.

**What changed in the runtime.** The operator authorized three bounded repairs inside this order, and the run needed all three:

- A source writer that dies while its resident survives is now replaced. The vertical host keeps the step pending only for a typed interruption while another launch is admitted (no dispatch hold or revocation, authority unexpired, the operator still away), and the source host's two-attempt bound and process-group proof end every path ([D003](../../evidence/WO-118/decisions.md#wo-118-d003--authorized-repairs-and-fresh-proof)). An invalid result, missing launch authority or the operator's return still ends the step. Under `dotln vertical` the same branch now leaves the step pending for a rerun instead of refusing the issue.
- The starter export carries the compiled `@dotln/console`, so an instance opens its live view without building: `node packages/console/dist/src/cli.js live --store .runtime/resident` ([kit README](../../../scripts/kit/README.client.md)). The first export of this order carried 866 manifest files and no console; the witnessed export of the reviewed subject verified 913.
- Live status shows resident-owned work. A running continuation step appears as a logical `vertical-host` actor and live episode whose launch claim source is `none`, an intent's decision hold appears as `intent-needs-human` or `intent-refused` qualified by its step without raw diagnostics, and a continuation whose last step settled terminally leaves the actor list while one that ended awaiting a decision keeps its hold ([D007](../../evidence/WO-118/decisions.md#wo-118-d007--live-vertical-status), [D012](../../evidence/WO-118/decisions.md#wo-118-d012--finished-continuations-leave-live-status)). The `runtime-status-v1` schema is unchanged.

The planning gate also admits one exact, operator-authorized status writeback to product 00's one-paragraph story. An amendment decision's `executionWritebacks` entry binds the reviewed and accepted section hashes to an unchanged reviewed criterion, and `npm run plan -- check` reports the change as `authorized-execution-writeback` ([D004](../../evidence/WO-118/decisions.md#wo-118-d004--exact-authorized-status-writeback); product 07 §Operator-opened planning pass).
