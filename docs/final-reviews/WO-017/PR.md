- **Store codec (item 1):** `decodeLog` and `appendEvent` accept only `""` or newline-terminated JSON-object lines and refuse a partial tail, a blank interior line, a whitespace-only log, an unterminated complete object, a non-object value, or invalid JSON with `Malformed JSONL at line N` followed by the detail; `eventId` is `evt_N` where N is the well-formed line count plus one, so a crash-shaped tail can no longer be counted, glued onto, or renumbered past. Seven tests, including a byte-exact append and round trip for well-formed logs.
- **Outbox ordering (item 2):** `replayOutbox` remembers a `CommandResult` that precedes its `CommandPersisted` by `commandId` and completes the command on persist with trace `["result", "preceded-persist"]`, leaving zero pending commands.
