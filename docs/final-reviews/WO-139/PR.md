# WO-139

On 2026-09-17 the operator watched a session whose top-level guideline was five agents reach more than a hundred through per-item review and refutation trees, and worked out the total by hand. This work order gives that fan-out a budget the hooks enforce wherever they can see a spawn. `docs/control/budgets.json` gains `subagentCap` (default 20; `null` disables); the Claude permission hook counts each root session's observed admissions and refuses the Agent or Task spawn that would exceed the cap, a Workflow call when no unit remains, and every tool call of an attributable child first seen beyond it, naming the count, the cap and the key. Stop and `harness usage` report count, cap and an uncounted remainder that is always `unknown`. Shared role text now requires planning the whole fan-out against the remaining budget before the first spawn, stating it, and batching review and refutation over item groups.

What it does not claim: this is an observed admission cap, not a guaranteed maximum. A workflow's agents exist before their first hook and count at their first attributable tool call; a tool-free agent is never seen; until an Agent result joins a direct admission to its child, overlap is reported as a minimum, so two unresolved admissions and two later children can be four agents counted as two; Codex fires no spawn hook and carries the cap as role text. Missing, unreadable, locked or invalid counter or budgets state admits with an advisory naming the cause. The open requirement stays in product 07's total-cap candidate with these observed paths, and the decisions record carries the per-harness, per-path admission table.
