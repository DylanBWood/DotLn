# WO-147

A resident that sees its host lock vanish between its presence check and its read now re-inspects the lock under the acquisition guard it already holds and continues, instead of ending the presence command or the resident transaction with a raw `ENOENT`. The guard excludes every new writer, so a lock that disappeared while it was held was released by its prior holder; absence proceeds, a present owner gets the same live, dead and shape judgments as before, and anything other than `ENOENT` still refuses. WO-143's verifier reproduced the failure two runs each on the working tree and on the released version and boarded it ahead of the first unattended hour; this order closes it.

**Why it is safe to merge.** No event, envelope, schema, predicate or guard record changes, a store written by v0.32.1 opens unchanged, the transaction's retry pattern is untouched so a missing log or a malformed lock still refuses, and `acquireGuard` is unchanged. The two-process fixture pauses a real contender after it observes `host.lock`, has the real holder release, and completes both direct acquisition and a resident transaction with prior event bytes identical; with the re-inspection removed it fails on raw `ENOENT`, reproduced by the executor and independently by the verifier. The retirement `ENOTEMPTY` inference did not reproduce in 20 deterministic attempts and is closed as unobserved, not disproved. Every WO-143 boundary fixture now takes its dead pid at use and asserts `ESRCH` before and after; the 344-boundary case that once failed under WO-069's gate passed ten runs under the runner's concurrent load and ten alone. The uncontended lock cycle is unchanged at about 17 to 19 ms median.

**What a reviewer should know.** The proof is bounded to writers that honor the acquisition guard; an out-of-protocol same-user process that creates or removes `host.lock` behind it is outside this order. `StorePathError` now carries the underlying filesystem code for every wrapped read, and only the acquisition catch reads it. The order edits a registered evidence source, so the authority and feedback evidence editions were re-minted as WO-147 revision 001 with one live `claude-cli-print` self-host audit at final review, after the verifier's post-pass correction [D008](docs/evidence/WO-147/decisions.md) found the review gate red on the stale editions; the feedback report differs from the previous edition in its subject hash only, and the choice to discharge that inside the review rather than through repair is recorded as [D010](docs/evidence/WO-147/decisions.md). `main` moved by one merge (WO-148, `v0.40.0`) while the order was in flight, so the branch was fast-forwarded and the target retimed from `v0.39.1` to `v0.40.1` under the unchanged patch classification, with no authored conflict and no byte under `packages/` differing from the verification checkpoint.

**The change itself.** Twenty-one lines in `packages/skeleton/src/worker-store.ts`: the lock inspection becomes a local operation invoked once and, on `ENOENT` only, once more under the same guard, and `atPath` copies the Node error code onto `StorePathError`. The pause-point peer learns `lstatSync`, `readFileSync` and `rmSync` pause points and a transaction mode; `resident.test.ts` gains the contention fixture, the retirement probe, the `assertDeadPid` helper and per-use pid allocation across the four WO-143 fixture families.
