## Goal alignment

Mission and critical path: this is operator-prioritized harness tooling, not a rung of the source-to-deliverable path. It contributes by removing the operator's hand translation of six role procedures into a third CLI, and by removing a live unknown — whether a Copilot session in a DotLn worktree was being denied every tool call, which nobody had observed. NoOp would have left both standing: the operator would keep the installed CLI unusable for DotLn work, or use it without knowing which controls held. The order's own framing is right that Branch C would have been a complete, smaller outcome; the probe landed better than that and the profile claims only what the probe showed.

Against the eight traps. **Rule beating** is the one this order is most exposed to, because every control is self-labelled: it is answered by requiring both a live denial and a fixture before any row reads enforced, by four rows meeting that bar and eleven not, and by the preamble that refuses to let "enforced" mean confinement. **Seeking the wrong goal** would be counting harness profiles; the order is judged on a complete executor/failing-verifier/fixer/passing-verifier workflow and on what stays untested, and the playbook says final review and release close are not qualified even though the same skills load. **Tragedy of the commons** is the real cost here and it is published rather than smoothed: 974 bytes of shared role text, a verifier ceiling raised one 4,096-byte step under the standing route, 196.35 AI credits and 1,088 s across four episodes, an operator-attested 8,000 of 20,000 monthly credits with this as the only Copilot workload, and a machinery gate 27 s slower across the combined integrations. **Escalation** is bounded by adding no suite, lane, gate, hook registration, dependency or agent. **Drift to low performance** is checked twice: VER-001 refused to let a red standing gate become a recorded limit, and D015 refuses to let a measured budget figure go stale. **Success to the successful** is answered by identical role bodies in both roots and by holding Copilot to the same evidence standard as Claude and Codex rather than granting a newcomer exception. **Shifting the burden to the intervenor** is visible and accepted: the operator runs the bare sessions and the four episodes, because no helper can observe a launch, and the records say attested where they are attested. **Policy resistance** cannot arise from one shared policy source; the design's decisive choice — probe before profile, and reuse the one registration — exists to avoid a second distribution of the same policy drifting from the first.
