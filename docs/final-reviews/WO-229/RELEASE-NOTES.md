## Release overview

Application v0.93.0 makes a gate say who it waited for and how long, without any checkout's path leaving its machine. The fork's planning pass of 2026-10-21 met a document gate whose summary read 304.86 s and then 1,520.76 s while every task ran under 6 s, WAIT lines that named holders without saying whose they were, and a planning condition that counted another checkout's complete run as the document gate's own cost. This release labels each holder's relation to the waiting checkout, carries the gate's lane wait apart from its work in the summary line and the recorded row, keeps that attribution past the 32-entry diagnostic history, reports an execution-only median beside the wall-clock one in the slow planning condition and marks contended samples, adds a read-only registry listing, and sizes the structural tasks' host requests from a measurement of their need.

Audience: operators who run several checkouts, worktrees or exported instances of one user on one machine and read a long gate wall time; planning passes that read the document-gate condition; and the fork, whose two timed-out-shaped gates this release explains.

## Read before upgrading

- **WAIT lines name a relation, never a path.** Each holder reads `this checkout`, `another checkout` or `checkout unknown` (a record without a worktree field, or one whose path no longer resolves), decided by comparing real paths with the waiting gate's own worktree. The record still holds the holder's path for that comparison; nothing prints or copies it.
- **The summary and the row carry the wait.** A gate summary ends with `; N s waiting for host lanes (… behind another checkout; …)` when the wait rounds above zero, computed as the union of the tasks' wait intervals so parallel waiting is counted once; relation parts can overlap when different holders block at the same time, and a part that rounds to zero is omitted. The recorded check row gains `hostLaneWait` (`wallMs`, `byRelation`, path-free `holders`) and each admitted task a compact `hostLaneWaitRelations` segment list beside its capped observations; the critical path reports `laneWaitMs` and `gapMs` whose sum is the former `waitMs`. Gate reuse strips every new field. Lock-phase time and time before the first capacity observation stay unknown; rows recorded before this release attribute only their retained observations.
- **The slow condition judges execution cost.** `npm run plan -- conditions --slow` reports, for the document gate, a wall median, an execution-only median (wall minus the recorded lane wait) and the contended sample count, judges the cost condition on the execution-only figure ([D001](../../evidence/WO-229/decisions.md#wo-229-d001--distinguish-execution-cost-and-experienced-delay)), binds each sample to its child gate by a unique marker, keeps a failed or timed-out sample unavailable with its contention when the row exists and does not retry, and shows live holders by relation in its host line. The 300 s budget is unchanged.
- **A read-only listing.** `node scripts/harness.mjs lanes` prints the recorded capacity and the live holders (relation, task, slots, age, nested, owner alive) and a transient-state caveat. It creates no registry, compiles no helper, takes no lock and reclaims no stale record; on macOS it needs the native process reader an ordinary gate prepares and otherwise reports `Host process reader unavailable`.
- **Structural host requests are measured.** At the recorded capacity of eight, `format` requests 3 host slots, `configuration-root`, `registrations`, `generated`, `host` and `kit-verify` 2 each, and `declarations` 4 under the operator-authorized exception ([D022](../../evidence/WO-229/decisions.md#wo-229-d022--authorize-declarations-at-its-measured-four-slot-need)); each still reserves all local lanes, so the local serial structural phase is unchanged, and a smaller host caps the request at its local reservation. Admission order is unchanged and no fair queue is added; a document gate beside a complete gate waits less, with no guarantee for every arrival or for older clients.
- **Versions.** Application v0.93.0 is a minor release over v0.92.0 with no component version change: console 0.4.6, skeleton 0.66.0, compiler 0.30.0, kernel 0.6.0, beacons 0.1.1 and browser-evidence 0.1.0 stay; no dependency is added; no role text changes.

## Substantive changes

**Host lanes** (`scripts/lib/host-lanes.mjs`). `checkoutRelation` compares real paths; `holderObservation` carries the relation and the diagnostic fields without the path; `formatLaneWait` prints the labelled WAIT line; `recordLaneWait` keeps the last 32 observations and every change of the observed relation set as a segment; `readHolders` lists the registry without the lock, without reclaiming and without preparing the reader, and the acquisition path keeps its own reclaiming reader.

**The gate** (`scripts/test-runner.mjs`, `scripts/lib/gate-timeline.mjs`, `scripts/lib/gate-reuse.mjs`). `gateHostLaneWait` unions the tasks' wait intervals and splits them by the segments; `formatHostLaneWait` renders the summary clause and omits zero-rounded figures; `gateCriticalPath` splits `waitMs` into `laneWaitMs` and `gapMs`; the runner records the segments through ordinary, `:overlap` and interrupted admissions, ends an outer reservation's wait at its acquisition so a later child inherits none of it, keeps an active admission's wait in a stopped row, records `planningSampleId` when a planning sample passes one, and requests `structuralHostSlots` for structural tasks and `kit-verify`; the reuse strip removes the new fields.

**Planning** (`scripts/lib/planning-conditions.mjs`). `measureDocumentGates` runs each slow sample as a child gate with a unique marker and reads its recorded row; `documentGateMeasurement` reports both medians and the judged figure; the host context carries the lane snapshot and the render shows it.

**Inspection and measurement** (`scripts/harness.mjs`, `scripts/measure-lanes.mjs`). `harness lanes` prints `readHolders` as JSON and the usage names it; the measurement script runs each structural task and `kit-verify` three times in core and in a fixture export under `time -lp`, records CPU, peak RSS, wall and load, and derives each request by the recorded rule.

**Tests.** The host-guard suite gains the relation-label case, the read-only listing case with a byte-identical registry, a real waiting gate with its summary and row, a stopped gate that keeps its active wait, a static holder beyond the heartbeat cap and rotating holders in a passed and a stopped gate; the gate-deadline suite gains the union and split arithmetic, the 80-change and late-join case, the zero-figure summary case, the overlap-boundary case and the sibling admission comparison with its full-capacity control; the plan-refutation suite gains the condition case with an unrelated concurrent row and the reuse strip; the runner suite extends its reuse case to the new fields.
