# WO-084

The idea ledger's header says a new session goes directly below it, never at the end of the file. On `main`, 31 sessions dated 2026-09-05 to 2026-09-18 sit below the old Resolutions section at line 6279 of 7,744, two of them operator ideation from 2026-09-18, where a newest-first reader does not meet them, and nothing checked where a section landed. This work order moves every dated session into newest-first order above the founding corpus, moves "Resolutions of known tensions" verbatim to [`docs/lineage/resolutions.md`](../../lineage/resolutions.md), and adds `node scripts/lineage.mjs index`, which generates [`docs/lineage/README.md`](../../lineage/README.md) (each section's line, entry and tag counts, totals by status, sections without a tag) and with `--check` refuses a section below the founding corpus, an undated or out-of-order heading, an entry with no lifecycle tag in its lead, a tag outside the declared set, and a stale index. The check and its seven fixtures run in `npm run test:docs` and stay out of `npm test`.

**Nothing was rewritten, and the planning receipts still resolve.** The ledger diff is 13,561 lines because it is a move; read it through the evidence, not the diff view. All 142 original sections survive as 141 plus Resolutions, every `##` heading is byte-identical, 138 bodies are byte-identical, two differ only in trailing blank lines, and two differ only by the six lead edits listed in the [migration manifest](../../evidence/WO-084/migration.json). VER-001 and the final review each established preservation with a script that shares no code with `lineage.mjs`.

**Two things here are the executor's reading of your vocabulary, and they are yours to confirm or correct.** First, the header's status declaration grows from five to ten, because 41 existing entries already use the other five and the check refuses an undeclared status. The new definitions read: `rejected` (declined with reasons), `deferred` (postponed with a reopening condition), `candidate` (proposed for future work), `open` (unresolved), `specified` (defined, not yet claimed implemented). The header adds that declaring them does not change any recorded disposition, declares `operator-directed` and `operator-authorized` as provenance, and drops the sentence that placed Resolutions at the bottom. Second, three entries gained an `adopted` tag they did not carry, because every entry needs a lifecycle status: in the 2026-09-06 WO-022 section, `**Recovered:**` became `` `adopted` `recovered`: `` and `**Implementation choice:**` became `` `adopted` **Implementation choice:** ``; in the 2026-09-08 planning section, "Two NoOps of the first result are reversed by operator direction" went from `` `operator-directed` `` to `` `adopted` `operator-directed` ``, matching every other operator-directed entry in that section. A correction to either is a header or tag edit, not a prose edit. The order's criterion 2 says the diff shows only moves, tag normalization and the header note; the final review judges the wider header change forced by the order's own objective and met in substance, and records it as an observation.
