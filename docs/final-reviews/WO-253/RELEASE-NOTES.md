## Release overview

DotLn v0.115.0 makes the kit clean up after itself in the minutes its own work takes. A test suite that passes removes the fixture roots it made as it ends, instead of leaving them for a prune; a prune judges a kit-owned temporary root by its owner record, with no file read, and reads the host's process table once for the whole run; the routine prune at `plan start` and at release close is a bounded step under a thirty-minute budget that stops, says what it removed and what remains with the command that finishes it, and lets the command go on; and a release close prunes only the closed order's own roots. The order is [WO-253](../../work-orders/WO-253-temporary-roots-leave-with-the-gate-that-made-them.md); it answers a planning pass that found `plan start` spending 0 h 17 min in the prune before opening its branch and a release close stopping in its prune on a restricted fixture directory after publishing in 0 h 2 min. Operators of a DotLn instance, and anyone whose host had gathered thousands of `dotln-*` roots, are the audience.

## Read before upgrading

- `docs/control/budgets.json` gains `prune.startBudgetMs`, the routine prune's wall budget at `plan start` and release close: 1,800,000 ms (0 h 30 min) when unset, unbounded when `null`. A value the budgets reader refuses (a negative number, a string, a `prune` that is not an object) leaves the default, and the command prints an advisory saying why; `npm run features` lists it as `prune-start-budget`. A backlog that outlasts the budget stops with `removed N; remaining M; resume with node scripts/harness.mjs prune --apply`, and that command, which sets no budget, finishes the remainder.
- Release close prunes only the closed order's own roots: those its worktree's ledger registered and its integration stashes. This checkout's other registered roots, other orders' retained lanes and the system temporary directory are left to the next `plan start` or to `node scripts/harness.mjs prune --apply`. The close record's `prune` step row carries `removed`, `remaining` and `stoppedAt`, and its summary prints a `Prune scope:` line and a `Close steps:` line with each step's active minutes.
- The test runner removes a passed suite's fixture roots as the suite ends and records `temporaryRootsRemoved` on the suite's row; a failed or stopped suite's roots are stamped `failed` and keep their 24-hour window from that moment. A fixture that read another suite's roots after that suite passed will no longer find them. The license check's npm dry-run snapshot is removed as the check returns, inside a gate too.
- A `disposable` declaration's last segment must hold a letter or digit: `*.*`, `**/*.*`, `.*`, `*-*` and `libs/*_*` are refused at registration, where `*`, `**`, `**/*` and `libs/**` already were, and `**/*.tsbuildinfo` stays admitted. A registration that carried such an entry refuses with the entry named until it is rewritten.
- `@dotln/skeleton` moves to 0.82.0 with the console's pin. Its runtime exports `removeTree`, `prepareTemporaryRemoval`, `restoreTemporaryRepairs`, `RESTRICTED_ENTRY`, `temporaryLedgerSegments` and `registeredTemporaryRootOf`; `makeTemporaryWritable` is no longer exported; `removeTemporaryRoot`, `removeVerifiedTemporaryRoot` and `retireTemporaryLedger` take an options argument (a budget and a `chmod` seam for the first two, a process table and a set of finished gate ids for the third). In `scripts/lib/temporary-prune.mjs`, `listTemporaryRoots`, `pruneBudget`, `PRUNE_BUDGET`, `PRUNE_LISTING_BATCH` and `PRUNE_PROGRESS_FLOOR_MS` are new and `temporaryPrunePaths` now serves fixtures only; `planHarnessPrune` is no longer exported from `scripts/lib/harness-prune.mjs`.
- The prune's report changes shape: each candidate names its `proof` (`record`, `inventory`, `legacy` or `remains`); a root judged on its record has `bytes: null` until its removal measures it, and the report and census count it as `unmeasured`; the report gains `remaining`, `unexamined`, `stoppedAt`, `budgetMs` and `reads` (process tables, writer views and byte inventories). A retained item the budget cut short carries `reason: "budget"` and `budget: true`.
- An entry inside a kit-owned root that its owner cannot read is granted `u+rwx` (a directory) or `u+rw` (a file) by an apply before removal, and a judgment that then keeps the root puts the grant back; an entry that cannot be repaired keeps its root as `restricted-entry` with the path, and an owner record the owner cannot read keeps its root by the record's path. None of these stops a prune or a close.
- The release-close and planner role roots each gain one sentence stating the close's prune scope and plan start's bounded step; the four roots already over their cold-start ceilings stay boarded ([D013](../../evidence/WO-253/decisions.md#wo-253-d013)).

## Substantive changes

**The runner removes what a passed suite made.** `scripts/test-runner.mjs` calls `finishGateTemporaryRoots` with the suite's name as each suite ends, records the count on the suite's row and reports every root removed during the gate at the gate's end; a suite-end cleanup that cannot run prints an advisory and leaves its roots to the gate's end, and a root the gate's end cannot list is a cleanup blocker, not a thrown error. `scripts/license-surfaces.mjs` removes its dry-run scratch with `removeTemporaryRoot` as it returns ([D003](../../evidence/WO-253/decisions.md#wo-253-d003--the-maker-of-the-leftover-license-surfaces-roots-a-deferred-teardown-and-gates-that-never-reached-their-end), [D004](../../evidence/WO-253/decisions.md#wo-253-d004--a-suites-end-removes-its-passed-roots-the-gates-end-reports-them-with-its-own)).

**The record is the proof.** `judgeTemporaryRoot` in `scripts/lib/temporary-prune.mjs` judges a root by its class, token, inode, ledger registration, owner liveness, outcome and window, and walks a root only once its record lets it go, for the owned roots nested in it; a retained root is never read. `scripts/lib/harness-prune.mjs` keeps the SHA-256 inventory for a `closed-journal` root past its window, whose proof is published beside its order's lane before the root goes, and for a lane, stash, snapshot, advisory or cache the plan will delete; a retained lane reads only its usage copies.

**One table, one index, one view a second.** The prune reads the process table once (`readProcesses` replaces the reader for a fixture) and passes it to every liveness judgment and to the ledger's retirement, which retires every finished gate's segments in one reading; the gate index is read once per repository per prune; a worktree's writer view is read only for a root it can protect and once per second of judging; the legacy rule's open-path observation is read once.

**A bounded, resumable step.** `pruneBudget` makes one wall budget per prune; `listTemporaryRoots` asks it every 64 entries and always lists its first batch; the temporary roots are judged one at a time, parents first, each removed on its judgment in an apply, the first root having the smaller of the budget and one second; the snapshots, advisories, claims, caches, lanes and stashes follow with what the budget leaves. Every walk, byte inventory and deletion asks the budget before each entry (`removeTree`, `prepareTemporaryRemoval`, `pruneInventory`, `laneUsageCopies`, `nestedTemporaryRoots`), and a stash's inventory before each tree listing and each batch of 32 objects; a shell claim's retirement and a stash's drop are asked about once before they start, and the ledger's retirement after an unstopped prune is unbounded closing bookkeeping. `formatPruneReport` prints `removed N; remaining M` with the resume command and where the budget stopped ([D014](../../evidence/WO-253/decisions.md#wo-253-d014--the-bounded-prune-judges-the-temporary-roots-first-and-its-listing-stays-inside-the-same-budget), [D039](../../evidence/WO-253/decisions.md#wo-253-d039--every-walk-and-deletion-of-a-planned-tree-asks-the-budget), [D041](../../evidence/WO-253/decisions.md#wo-253-d041--round-1-the-stash-inventory-asks-the-budget-and-the-verifiers-dispatch-lines-are-corrected)).

**Plan start and the close.** `scripts/refute-plan.mjs` wraps each startup step in a timer that prints the step and its duration in hours and minutes, and runs the prune with the budget `pruneStartBudget` reads, printing its advisory when the key is refused. `scripts/release.mjs` loads the prune module before removing the worktree, prunes with `scope: "close"` and the order's captured roots under the same budget, records the prune row through `recordPruneStep` and prints the scope and steps lines; what a failed prune removed before it failed stays recorded. `scripts/lib/temporary-prune-summary.mjs` gives a failed apply's census what the budget leaves.

**Carried follow-ups.** `releaseCloseSummary` names kept starter seeds with their reasons beside published ones, and `validateDisposablePaths` in `scripts/lib/config.mjs` requires a letter or digit in the last segment's literal part ([D011](../../evidence/WO-253/decisions.md#wo-253-d011--the-carried-follow-ups-kept-starter-seeds-printed-and-the-disposable-floor-needs-a-letter-or-digit)).

**Guides, role text and tests.** The kit guide's §Owned cleanup and retention and product 07's §Workflow closeout and releases, §Operator recovery controls, plan-start paragraph and fixture-lifetime paragraphs state the suite-end removal, the record proof, the bounded prune with its key and resume, the close's scope and the timing lines as built. `scripts/test-temporary-roots.mjs` adds nineteen cases (the record judgment with unreadable and mode-000 entries, the unrepairable entry, the one-millisecond and 2,000-root budgets, the listing's batches, the stopped snapshot, lane, stash, cache and temporary-root deletions, the grants put back, the queued nested roots, the sealed owner record, the gate's-end blocker, the preview's unmeasured count, the failed apply's census and the budget-key rule); `scripts/test-fixture-temp-root.sh` counts one table over ten roots and two writer views over twenty; `scripts/test-runner.test.mjs`, `scripts/test-plan-refutation.mjs`, `scripts/test-release.sh`, `scripts/test-license-surfaces.mjs`, `scripts/test-harness.mjs`, `scripts/test-configuration-root.mjs` and `scripts/test-launchpad-update.mjs` carry the suite-end, plan-start, close, license, default-budget, floor and kept-seed cases.

## Progressive polish

`formatPruneReport` prints `(N unmeasured)` beside a byte count; `npm run features` renders the budget in hours and minutes; `removalOptions` builds both removals' options once; `temporaryContext` and `pruneContext` separate what a temporary root's judgment reads from what the planned kinds' judgments read, and the ref-backend read waits for a stash; the docblocks on `pruneUnderLock`, `planHarnessPrune`, `judgeTemporaryRoot`, `applyPlannedCandidate` and the walk options say what each walk asks and what a stop leaves; `scripts/lib/gate-reuse.mjs` strips a reused task's suite-end fields; the sixteen-hook runtime snapshot and the harness manifest are regenerated for the runtime sources' new content hash; the verifier's VER-001 records D032 to D037 gain the control prefix the document check requires. The small inaccuracies recorded and not fixed are listed in the [handoff](../../evidence/WO-253/handoff.md).
