# WO-253 — Plan start and release close finish in their own minutes: a passed suite removes its fixtures as it ends, kit-owned roots are pruned by their records under a bounded, resumable budget, and the close prunes only its order's roots

Until now the kit's temporary roots left the host only when a prune paid for them. A passed suite's fixture roots waited for the gate's end, and five WO-241 gates stopped before their end left 1,299 of the 1,431 `dotln-license-surfaces-*` roots this host still holds. For every root it judged, the prune built a fresh context, read every worktree's writer view (a `ps` listing each) and parsed a 32 MB gate index, and it hashed every file of every lane, snapshot and stash it judged. On 2026-11-03 `plan start` spent 0 h 17 min reaching its branch while removing 465 of 5,988 roots, and WO-241's close published both releases in 0 h 2 min, then stopped in its prune on a restricted fixture directory, and the host-wide prune the session ran next took another 0 h 16 min before it was stopped ([the order's observed gap](../../work-orders/WO-253-temporary-roots-leave-with-the-gate-that-made-them.md), [D002](../../evidence/WO-253/decisions.md#wo-253-d002--the-prune-never-hashed-a-temporary-root-what-it-repeated-per-root-was-the-context-the-writer-views-and-the-gate-index), [D003](../../evidence/WO-253/decisions.md#wo-253-d003--the-maker-of-the-leftover-license-surfaces-roots-a-deferred-teardown-and-gates-that-never-reached-their-end)).

Now the runner removes a passed suite's roots as the suite ends and records the count on its row, a failed or stopped suite's roots keep their 24-hour window from that moment, and the license check removes its own npm dry-run snapshot as it returns ([D004](../../evidence/WO-253/decisions.md#wo-253-d004--a-suites-end-removes-its-passed-roots-the-gates-end-reports-them-with-its-own)). A prune judges a kit-owned root of class `gate-fixture`, `warm-fixture`, `worker-capture` or `session-scratch` on its owner record alone, with no file read, and names `record` as the proof; the byte inventory is kept only for a `closed-journal` root past its window, whose proof is written beside its order's lane, and for a lane, stash, snapshot, advisory or cache the plan will delete. One process-table reading serves every owner-liveness judgment, a worktree's writer view is read once a second rather than once a root, and the gate index once a repository. The routine prune at `plan start` and at the close is a bounded step under `prune.startBudgetMs` in `docs/control/budgets.json`, 1,800,000 ms (0 h 30 min) when unset and unbounded when null: the budget is asked between roots and inside every listing, walk, byte inventory and deletion, an item it cuts short is kept as `budget`, the apply prints `removed N; remaining M; resume with node scripts/harness.mjs prune --apply` and where it stopped, and the command goes on ([D014](../../evidence/WO-253/decisions.md#wo-253-d014--the-bounded-prune-judges-the-temporary-roots-first-and-its-listing-stays-inside-the-same-budget), [D039](../../evidence/WO-253/decisions.md#wo-253-d039--every-walk-and-deletion-of-a-planned-tree-asks-the-budget)). The close prunes only the closed order's own roots, records `removed`, `remaining` and `stoppedAt` on its `prune` step row and prints a `Prune scope:` line and a `Close steps:` line; `plan start` prints one `<step>: <h> h <m> min` line for each of failures, reader scores, conditions, census, prune and branch. An entry inside a kit-owned root that its owner cannot read is granted `u+rwx` or `u+rw` before removal, a judgment that keeps the root puts its grants back, and an entry that cannot be repaired keeps its root as `restricted-entry` with the path; none of these stops a prune or a close.
