# Re-mining Well

Revisit original sources, compare what survived into the product, and turn
promising differences into concrete scenes and experiments. A draw is finite;
the possible meaning of its sources is not. An atlas is a lossy projection,
not a substitute for its detailed research records.

## Draw records and preservation

Each dated run lives under `runs/draw-NNN/`. Its atlas and coverage report are
immutable after close. Detailed research records preserve every distinct
captured idea, including useful alternatives, rejected options, uncertainties,
and deferred leads. Ranking selects work; it never deletes research.

Before moving from research to synthesis, persist each distinct idea with a
stable ID, source and access limit, source meaning, original application,
concrete scene, destination, constraints, experiment and falsifier. Keep audit
corrections next to the affected idea. A short handoff or context summary is
only an index into these files and must never replace them. Newly discovered
ideas receive new IDs; a merger preserves the old IDs and their differences.
Record unsampled material as a lead, not as a finding. The atlas card budget
does not cap the number of ideas preserved in the detailed research track.

Raw source paths, hashes, locators, images and observation notes remain in the
main checkout's ignored intake. Public records use rewritten prose and opaque
local-source references. External research uses ordinary public citations.
Operator ideation, unendorsed model proposals, visual observation, uncertain
OCR, prior ledger interpretation and current product interpretation remain
distinct strata. Descendants of one source do not independently corroborate it.

## Source treatment

Preserve the portable capability behind a source example, including examples
drawn from a prior implementation. Rebuild its expression from product intent.
An automatic enterprise-tracker import remains a possible use case with
interchangeable connectors; a private deployment example does not limit the
idea to that deployment. Remove private names, configuration, identifiers,
implementation details and secrets from the synthesis. Do not reproduce or
derive an implementation from employer code. The operator's draw-001
disposition permits this granular generalization; it supersedes blanket
exclusion of a whole mixed note for that draw.

## Reading and coverage

Declare sources, snapshot, lenses, scales and budget before each pass. Preserve
the first-contact shape before reading descendants when practical; otherwise
record the prior visibility. Inspect images as compositions and meaningful
regions. Compare source → ledger → product → executable evidence, then travel
from a felt scene down to an experiment and back up to check what was lost.

Every declared coverage cell receives one terminal disposition: `yield`,
`reinforcement`, `represented-elsewhere`, `duplicate-with-lineage`, `tension`,
`no-yield`, `unreadable`, `missing-context`, `quarantined`, or
`deferred-with-reason`. Deferred cells state their reopening condition.
Incomplete work stays explicitly incomplete until the pass is finished.
A yield is missing, orphaned or flattened meaning that survives a counterlens;
an implementation gap in an already retained idea is separately reported.

Rule-bearing findings name the cheapest sufficient mechanism, a plausible
rejected mechanism, and a consumer. Discovery does not equip a policy or widen
authority. Ledger integration, product promotion and implementation follow
their authorized orders. The draw proposes specific destinations and acceptance
scenes without silently changing settled decisions.

## Lenses

The pilot uses first-contact witness; skeptic and falsifier; affected party and
newcomer; and environment-bound implementer. Later draws may choose from the
full pack: Romantic witness/poet; curious player/child; game/toy designer; stage
director/social dramatist; resident/affected-party advocate; gardener/ecologist/
systems thinker; archaeologist/historian/provenance keeper; maximalist world
builder; minimalist/subtractor; classical systems mechanic/compiler; skeptic/
falsifier; accessibility/privacy newcomer; environment-bound implementer.
These are reading postures, not actor identities or grants of authority.
