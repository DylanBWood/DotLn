## QU-01 — Retain capability after a recoverable failure

**Source meaning.** Taleb distinguishes resistance to disturbances from benefit
through them, favors small recoverable mistakes that supply information, and
warns that gains can conceal losses imposed elsewhere. Taleb–Douady formalize
sensitivity to dispersion and nonlinear response.
[Prologue](https://www.fooledbyrandomness.com/prologue.pdf),
[mathematical abstract](https://arxiv.org/abs/1208.1189).

**Application and scene.** A worker encounters expired evidence, stops safely,
and produces a reproduction and proposed correction. Reviewed corrections give
future disposable workers durable capability. Target workshop, correction
compiler and workstream learning, with existing containment/replay invariants.

**Experiment and falsifier.** Introduce stale evidence, interruption and a
misleading recommendation in a sandbox. Compare a baseline with retained,
reviewed corrections on held-out variants. Repeated mistakes/recovery must
decrease without greater new failures. Mere survival is robustness, not evidence
of improvement. Include operator recovery/review time and affected artifacts in
the losses; do not disturb real users or external systems to prove a metaphor.

**Route.** Build on existing replay/feedback contracts. WO-039/040 address actual
session governance; the continuing correction path remains a distinct
application opportunity (see OF-02). This idea is an evaluation lens, not a new
kernel property inferred from a book title.

## QU-02 — Preserve a quality judgment before demanding a rule

**Source meaning.** Pirsig recounts students comparing writing quality before
defining it satisfactorily. His later essay treats judgment as central to the
inquiry begun in _Zen_. This is philosophical argument/classroom recollection,
not a universal measurement theory.
[Essay, discussion corresponding to pages 11–13](https://www.original.moq.org/forum/Pirsig/emm.html).

**Application and scene.** Two valid resume screens expose the same facts; one
makes the next action clearer. Accept a preference, marked moment and context
before asking for an explanation. Offer a provisional distinction later for
review. Target operator comparison, experience capture and correction authoring.

**Experiment and falsifier.** Compare rubric-first and pairwise review across
eight product choices; revisit some later. Useful subsequent selection should
improve. Strong presentation-order effects, unpredictable reversals or more
capture effort than benefit challenge the approach. Taste can be contextual or
biased; preference does not prove correctness, change authority or override an
invariant. Do not infer a diagnosis from hesitation.

**Route.** Add pairwise judgments to the behavior/configuration comparison lab.
Retain examples without automatically promoting their interpretation into a rule.

## QU-03 — Compose ideas into scenes before forcing a fixed outline

**Source meaning.** In his authorized 1974 talk, Pirsig describes movable idea
slips and reorganization as understanding changed. GEB's publisher separately
describes dialogues that demonstrate their concepts structurally. That latter
claim remains publisher-level grounding.
[Pirsig talk](https://lithub.com/robert-m-pirsig-on-the-book-he-wrote-and-the-one-he-didnt/),
[GEB description](https://www.hachettebookgroup.com/titles/douglas-r-hofstadter/g%C3%B6del-escher-bach/9780465026562/).

**Application and scene.** Combine disposable workers, restored intent and
compiled corrections into an interruption/resumption scene. Reordering or
replacing an idea changes what happens and reveals a missing handoff rule. Ideas
gain scenes, constraints, alternatives and experiments before settled specs.
Target the ideation/planning interface and executable pattern workshop.

**Experiment and falsifier.** Five dormant ideas, two competing scenes, existing
replay fixtures and a lightweight interface. Require a decision, discriminating
experiment or explicit deferral that ordinary review cannot deliver as cheaply.
Card rearrangement alone can postpone work. Label each behavior executable,
simulated or prose-only so a persuasive demonstration cannot conceal assumptions.

**Route.** A workshop/content seed for the temporal lab and ledger-promotion
problem. QU-12 separately tests alternative representations; composition and
representation are related, but neither erases the other record.

## QU-04 — Connect self-description to independently inspectable execution

**Source meaning.** Hofstadter's later abstract identifies the human self as
GEB's central intuition; documented contents include formal systems, meaning,
levels and self-reference. Book text remains unread, so this is a conceptual
lead, not a proof or a consciousness claim.
[Author abstract](https://www.thestrangeloop.com/2013/what-is-a-strange-loop-and-what-is-it-like-to-be-one.html),
[contents](https://utahbookmobiles.aspendiscovery.org/GroupedWork/caedfdd3-1346-4411-8b86-b039ee137283-eng/Home).

**Application and scene.** An actor explains that it stopped for missing
authority. The operator follows the statement to the check, event and stopped
action. Competing interpretations cite the same preserved history. Target
explanation, provenance and counterfactual views.

**Experiment and falsifier.** Show one execution through event, actor and
operator views; plant three narrative/trace contradictions. Failure to locate
them, or divergence with no identifiable transformation/missing observation,
falsifies the inspector. Replay establishes recorded behavior under recorded
conditions, not the truth or causal force of every explanation. Do not add a
strange-loop type or assert actor selfhood. Deeper GEB reading remains required
before making GEB-specific technical claims.

## QU-05 — Use quick recognition to direct inspection

**Source meaning.** _Blink_ combines rapid selective judgment with failures;
_Talking to Strangers_ challenges confidence about unfamiliar people. Kahneman
and Klein emphasize environmental regularity and learning opportunity in human
expertise, rather than subjective certainty.
[Blink introduction](https://gladwell.com/books/blink/),
[Strangers introduction](https://gladwell.com/books/talking-to-strangers/),
[2009 paper abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19739881/).

**Application and scene.** An operator marks a result that looks wrong. Show the
changed artifact, observed cues and missing evidence, keeping interpretation
separate. Do not infer dishonesty from terse language or confidence from fluent
text. Target preflight and review surfaces; transfer of human findings to models
is unproven.

**Experiment and falsifier.** On twenty known-defect artifacts compare a compact
cue view with normal review; measure detection, false alarms and time before
showing explanations. Faster review that misses consequential defects fails.
Apparent confidence must not replace relevant cues. This is an attention aid,
not an automatic authority or truth judgment.

## QU-06 — Compare workers and the conditions enabling their success

**Source meaning.** _Outliers_ emphasizes enabling circumstances. Gladwell later
rejects a universal sufficient-hours interpretation and emphasizes access,
preparation and variation. A later violinist replication found a substantial
but smaller practice relationship without reproducing every original group
separation; it supplies no universal threshold.
[Author description](https://gladwell.com/books/outliers),
[2013 clarification](https://www.newyorker.com/sports/sporting-scene/complexity-and-the-ten-thousand-hour-rule),
[Macnamara–Maitra study](https://artscimedia.case.edu/wp-content/uploads/sites/141/2019/10/03143050/Macnamara-Maitra-2019.pdf).

**Application and scene.** One worker receives an executable example; another
receives an underspecified ticket artifact. A crossover separates scaffold
benefit from worker configuration. Record task clarity, evidence, corrections,
time, tools and feedback alongside outcomes. Target evaluation, work-order
design and pattern selection.

**Experiment and falsifier.** Cross two worker configurations with two evidence
packages on matched tasks at fixed permissions/budget. Scaffolding must improve
held-out results enough to maintain it; retain configuration differences that
survive the crossover. Workers are not assumed interchangeable, and model
rankings should not be hardcoded from a favorable context.

## QU-07 — Let constraints change strategy

**Source meaning.** Gladwell's _David and Goliath_ interview frames advantage as
dependent on a conflict's rules, distinguishes potentially useful difficulty
from harm and acknowledges revisions of earlier claims.
[Author interview](https://knowledge.wharton.upenn.edu/article/david-goliath-malcolm-gladwell-advantages-disadvantages/).

**Application and scene.** A short-lived worker with precise evidence and one
correction may outperform a broad-context worker on a bounded repair; another
task needs wider context. Preserve that boundary rather than ranking every
strategy on one resource ladder. Target decomposition and execution-pattern
trials.

**Experiment and falsifier.** Twelve bounded tasks, broad/narrow strategies,
equal total resources. Count handoff failures, omitted requirements and operator
intervention, not just local savings. Report losses as well as wins. Survivor
stories do not establish universal benefit; imposed difficulty must not become
a prescription for deprivation, disability or operator frustration.

## QU-08 — Expose how agreement and patterns spread

**Source meaning.** _The Tipping Point_ proposes differing effects of
participants, message presentation and context; _Revenge_ considers harmful
spread and shared narratives. Watts–Dodds' simulations offer a counterlens in
which network susceptibility can matter more than exceptional influencers.
[Licensed chapter excerpt](https://www.bookbrowse.com/excerpts/index.cfm/book_number/513/page_number/2/The-Tipping-Point),
[Revenge publisher](https://www.gladwellbooks.com/titles/malcolm-gladwell/revenge-of-the-tipping-point/9780316575805/),
[author interview](https://hbr.org/podcast/2024/10/malcolm-gladwells-new-take-on-tipping-points),
[primary simulation paper](https://pdodds.w3.uvm.edu/research/papers/others/2007/watts2007a.pdf).

**Application and scene.** Five actors endorse a proposal, but every citation
leads to one mistaken summary. Compare exposure order, communication structure
and independent evidence. Show adoption history for reviewed patterns. Target
community counterfactuals and promotion; popularity is not quality.

**Experiment and falsifier.** Six actors, one seeded false claim, two structures;
repeat with reversed exposure and independent evidence. Provenance must separate
independent support from copying. Different wording with the same unsupported
premise is not evidence diversity. Model-based social results are not universal
laws and cannot simply be assigned to software actors. Retain this research
seed; defer production rules about special proportions or personality roles.

## QU-09 — Choose between more evidence and a better interpretation

**Source meaning.** In _Open Secrets_, Gladwell credits Gregory Treverton with
the puzzle/mystery distinction: missing information versus interpretation under
uncertainty. More disclosure does not necessarily supply understanding. This
is Treverton's distinction applied by Gladwell.
[Original article, January 2007](https://www.newyorker.com/magazine/2007/01/08/open-secrets-3).

**Application and scene.** A stalled workstream identifies either a missing
constraint or competing interpretations of available material. Each
interpretation says what evidence would change it. Research support/workshop
can propose retrieval, reinterpretation or a discriminating test; the task may
change modes and need not receive a permanent kernel classification.

**Experiment and falsifier.** Twelve original synthetic cases include decisive
missing facts and adequate but poorly organized evidence. Compare retrieval-first
with adaptive next-action selection at equal budget. Measure actual blockage
resolution and unnecessary retrieval. Mixed cases or classifier cost may defeat
a mandatory taxonomy. Interpretation is never permission to invent evidence.

## QU-10 — Match recovery help to an observable failure

**Source meaning.** _The Art of Failure_ contrasts intrusive conscious control
with narrowed attention/unavailable responses, drawing on Daniel Willingham and
Ephimia Morphew. Similar-looking human failures can require different help;
the essay does not diagnose software workers.
[Original article, August 2000](https://www.newyorker.com/magazine/2000/08/21/the-art-of-failure).

**Application and scene.** One worker lacks a prerequisite; another loops
through redundant checks despite sufficient evidence. Compare a prerequisite
procedure, a simplified support preserving requirements, and a fresh worker
with compact handoff. State the trace feature addressed. Target worker supports
and compiled corrections, without psychological labels.

**Experiment and falsifier.** Fixtures deliberately contain known prerequisite
gaps or redundant support instructions. Measure completion, operator repair,
resources and violations for the three interventions. If one cheap default wins,
an automatic dispatcher is needless. Defer dispatch until enough reproducible
failures exist; preserve the intervention alternatives as content now.

## QU-11 — Test the intended benefit separately from technical success

**Source meaning.** In his May 3, 2021 PBS conversation with Walter Isaacson,
Gladwell describes the Bomber Mafia's humane technical ambition, idealized
conditions and the gap between intention and use, relating it to technological
optimism. This is substantive author interpretation, not a historical causal
audit or a DotLn effect estimate.
[Interview and transcript](https://www.pbs.org/wnet/amanpour-and-company/video/malcolm-gladwell-and-the-bomber-mafia/).

**Application and scene.** A technically successful automation transfers
supervision and repair to the operator. Before promotion, record intended
benefit, mechanism, necessary conditions and observed consequences separately.
Fewer interruptions and more autonomous calls are different claims. Record who
bears repair cost. Target experiments, review and operator feedback.

**Experiment and falsifier.** Compare proposed/current workflows on the same
task bundle. Predeclare accepted results, total operator time including
supervision/repair, and reversals after apparent completion. A technical metric
improving without the intended benefit fails the promotion claim. The historical
narrative cannot determine the effects or tradeoffs of the proposed automation.

## QU-12 — Compare alternative representations of an idea

**Source meaning.** Chalmers, French and Hofstadter argue that representations
involve relevance selection and organization; preselecting them can hide a hard
part of a task. Their Copycat discussion uses a restricted domain. The inspected
document is a 1991 technical-report version of the 1992 paper, not GEB text.
Forbus, Gentner, Markman and Ferguson challenge aspects of the architecture and
defend separating mapping from other processes.
[High-Level Perception, Representation, and Analogy](https://cse.msu.edu/~cse841/papers/hofstader.pdf),
[original researchers' response](https://www.qrg.northwestern.edu/papers/Files/chalmers/response-Chalmers-et-al-JETAI.html).

**Application and scene.** One idea packet can become an actor practice,
reusable correction, product interaction or executable scene. The workshop
offers materially different organizations with evidence links, omissions,
assumptions and first runnable scenes. The operator compares what each enables
before adopting one. Preserve provenance/authority while changing representation.

**Experiment and falsifier.** Twelve original packets, equal total budgets:
three paraphrases in a standard task frame versus three materially different
representations. Evaluate intent/constraint preservation, useful scenes and
downstream prototype quality. More variants or more creative language without
better executable outcomes fails. Test representations without importing either
contested cognitive theory as kernel architecture.

## Combined seeds and preserved deferrals

Retain idea-to-scene composition (QU-03), judgment-to-correction (QU-02), failure
leaving a regression (QU-01), worker/scaffold crossover (QU-06/07), and community
diffusion (QU-08). Combine representation comparison (QU-12) and evidence-demand
selection (QU-09) for a promotion experiment; evaluate its actual benefit with
QU-11. QU-10's recovery alternatives remain useful before any automatic dispatch.

The small experiments are decision screens, not broad proof. Positive results
justify larger held-out trials. GEB's book text/self-reference/consciousness
themes and the forthcoming Gladwell book remain reading leads. The anthology
and Bomber Mafia have substantive but partial coverage, not full-book coverage.
No magic group proportions, universal practice-hour thresholds, fixed model
rankings or philosophical kernel categories are promoted.
