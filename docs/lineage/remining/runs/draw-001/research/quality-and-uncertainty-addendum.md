# Quality and uncertainty — breadth addendum

Research date: 2026-09-07. QUA-01–QUA-22 and the named amendments preserve
additional ideas and experiments omitted by the initial shortlist. Substantive
means relevant author text or primary research was inspected; discovery-only
means a reading lead. Every DotLn application remains an untested original
hypothesis. No new browsing or experiments were performed for this addendum.

## Additional ideas and experimental branches

### QUA-01 — Map exposure without predicting the next failure

**Grounding:** Taleb's prologue distinguishes disturbance sensitivity from
forecasting a particular disturbance. Its stronger universal risk claims are not
adopted. [Prologue](https://www.fooledbyrandomness.com/prologue.pdf).
**Scene/layer:** the workshop shows how outcomes change with older evidence,
more interruptions or unavailable dependencies, instead of assigning speculative
probabilities. **Experiment:** sweep severity while holding the task fixed;
record accepted output, recovery and violations. **Falsifier:** the map misses
held-out weaknesses or costs more than the failure it prevents. This differs
from QU-01's retained correction after an actual failure.

### QUA-02 — Keep a working baseline while trying bounded alternatives

**Grounding:** the sampled Taleb prologue names a barbell approach and contrasts
small reversible errors with rare irreversible ones. This does not reconstruct
its full strategy chapter. [Prologue](https://www.fooledbyrandomness.com/prologue.pdf).
**Scene/layer:** a proven pattern serves work while a separate workshop budget
tests alternatives. **Experiment:** compare one speculative redesign with a
baseline plus isolated variants at equal total budget. **Falsifier:** added
coordination yields neither useful alternatives nor dependable output. No
percentage split is established; experiments earn promotion through evidence.

### QUA-03 — Try subtraction before adding another intervention

**Grounding:** discovery only; the via-negativa book treatment was not inspected.
[Reference edition](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/176227/antifragile-by-nassim-nicholas-taleb/hardcover/).
**Scene/layer:** support maintenance considers adding a rule, simplifying it or
removing a redundant step, while preserving locked requirements.
**Experiment:** compare those three candidates with unchanged behavior on one
recurring failure. **Falsifier:** omission/repair rises. This extends beyond
QU-10's recovery-specific simplification to routine support evolution.

### QUA-04 — Capture an idea where actual use makes it meaningful

**Grounding:** Pirsig's 1974 talk contrasts idealized writing conditions with an
idea arising through ongoing activity; autobiographical testimony, not a law.
[Authorized talk](https://lithub.com/robert-m-pirsig-on-the-book-he-wrote-and-the-one-he-didnt/).
**Scene/layer:** during resume/review/handoff, mark the moment needing a better
design; retain situation, desired difference and artifact, elaborate later.
**Experiment:** compare a week of situated captures with retrospective ideation
at similar effort. **Falsifier:** capture disrupts work, produces unusable
fragments or adds no scene quality. This is acquisition before composition.

### QUA-05 — Revise or retire patterns after contradicting experience

**Grounding:** Pirsig's later essay contrasts ongoing experience with retained
value patterns, a philosophical lens rather than software taxonomy.
[Essay](https://www.original.moq.org/forum/Pirsig/emm.html).
**Scene/layer:** a correction passes its original regression but harms new tasks.
Preserve the counterexample, then narrow, supersede or retire with review.
**Experiment:** change task conditions; compare permanent accumulation with
reviewed revision. **Falsifier:** revision destabilizes valid behavior or loses
the earlier valid cases. Library growth alone is not learning.

### QUA-06 — Compare presentation by correct reuse

**Grounding:** The Tipping Point excerpt considers presentation's contribution
to memorability/influence.
[Chapter excerpt](https://www.bookbrowse.com/excerpts/index.cfm/book_number/513/page_number/5/the-tipping-point).
**Scene/layer:** show one correction as explanation, trigger/action rule or
worked example plus counterexample. **Experiment:** hold content fixed and test
unfamiliar applicable/inapplicable cases. **Falsifier:** memorable presentation
loses qualifications or increases false application. Measure reuse, not clicks
or agreement. This differs from QU-08's diffusion provenance.

### QUA-07 — Separate shared visibility from accepted responsibility

**Grounding:** the dated excerpt attributes diffusion-of-responsibility
experiments to Bibb Latané and John Darley; no agent psychology follows.
[Chapter excerpt](https://www.bookbrowse.com/excerpts/index.cfm/book_number/513/page_number/6/The-Tipping-Point).
**Scene/layer:** several actors see a blocker, but none accepts its next action.
Distinguish visible, acknowledged and accepted responsibility in coordination.
**Experiment:** compare broadcast and explicit acceptance, including worker
disappearance. **Falsifier:** added delay or ambiguous duplicate ownership
without less unattended work. Visibility/endorsement grants no authority.

### QUA-08 — Evaluate a correction curriculum by transfer

**Grounding:** original branch from Gladwell's clarification and the inspected
practice replication; preparation/variation do not imply universal thresholds.
[Clarification](https://www.newyorker.com/sports/sporting-scene/complexity-and-the-ten-thousand-hour-rule),
[replication](https://artscimedia.case.edu/wp-content/uploads/sites/141/2019/10/03143050/Macnamara-Maitra-2019.pdf).
**Scene/layer:** examples progress from familiar to changed surfaces to different
applications. **Experiment:** repeated identical regressions versus varied
examples at equal evaluation budget. **Falsifier:** improvement only on seen
fixtures. The maintained example/correction system changes; worker weights are
not assumed to learn during use.

### QUA-09 — Distinguish learning friction from obstruction

**Grounding:** Gladwell's interview credits Robert/Elizabeth Bjork for desirable
difficulties and distinguishes benefit from harm; underlying studies were not
independently read. [Interview](https://knowledge.wharton.upenn.edu/article/david-goliath-malcolm-gladwell-advantages-disadvantages/).
**Scene/layer:** in a voluntary workshop exercise, predict an artifact change
or identify a constraint before seeing the worked answer. **Experiment:** compare
direct instruction with a reconstruction step and test later transfer.
**Falsifier:** extra work without durable benefit. This is not an obstacle added
to ordinary operator work; learning friction differs from a resource constraint.

### QUA-10 — Make retraction travel through dependent artifacts

**Grounding:** original branch from author revision and harmful-diffusion lenses.
[Interview](https://knowledge.wharton.upenn.edu/article/david-goliath-malcolm-gladwell-advantages-disadvantages/),
[Revenge description](https://www.gladwellbooks.com/titles/malcolm-gladwell/revenge-of-the-tipping-point/9780316575805/).
**Scene/layer:** retract a popular correction and inspect dependent supports,
decisions/artifacts as requires-review, known-invalid or unaffected.
**Experiment:** seed one faulty premise across dependents, retract, and measure
impact completeness/precision. **Falsifier:** missed dependents or indiscriminate
invalidation. History remains; its present standing changes.

### QUA-11 — Try local proposals before one large interpretation

**Grounding:** later Copycat research describes competing structures and
stochastic local exploration in a restricted domain.
[Paper](https://cse.msu.edu/~cse841/papers/hofstader.pdf).
**Scene/layer:** bounded proposals group observations, reinterpret a dependency
or produce a counterexample before choosing a larger workshop organization.
**Experiment:** monolithic versus budget-matched local proposals with explicit
selection. **Falsifier:** redundant commentary/selection cost without better
scenes. This is an alternative, not a kernel transplant.

### QUA-12 — Check whether a fixture already supplies the hard answer

**Grounding:** the representation critique challenges human-prepared relevance
choices; responding researchers dispute parts of it. Make it an audit question,
not a rejection of structured input.
[Critique](https://cse.msu.edu/~cse841/papers/hofstader.pdf),
[response](https://www.qrg.northwestern.edu/papers/Files/chalmers/response-Chalmers-et-al-JETAI.html).
**Scene/layer:** success on a curated summary fails on the ordinary source
artifact. **Experiment:** compare prepared, ordinary and independently prepared
representations; identify which workflow stage actually solves the task.
**Falsifier:** no material difference or disproportionate cost. Scaffolding may
still be valuable; describe the complete system's performance accurately.

### QUA-13 — Inspect opportunity allocation after an early winner

**Grounding:** original extrapolation from Outliers' opportunity lens; no
software-allocation mechanism was established.
[Author description](https://gladwell.com/books/outliers).
**Scene/layer:** an early winner receives better examples/feedback and dominates
later partly for that reason. **Experiment:** greedy selection versus a small
continuing budget for matched trials, preserving evidence/support availability.
**Falsifier:** trials do not improve decisions enough to pay their cost. This
differs from QU-06's one-time crossover; compare also MS-10 without counting the
two sources as independent proof of the proposed application.

## Amendments attached to earlier records

- **QUA-A1 → QU-01, cost bearers.** Retain who benefits, performs recovery and
  bears artifact consequences. Compare gross completion with supervision,
  repair and review included. [Taleb prologue](https://www.fooledbyrandomness.com/prologue.pdf).
- **QUA-A2 → QU-02, contextual disagreement.** Preserve purpose, comparison set,
  evaluator and conditions where a preference reverses. Test contextual
  prediction against an aggregate quality ranking; do not keep only the winner.
  [Pirsig essay](https://www.original.moq.org/forum/Pirsig/emm.html),
  [talk](https://lithub.com/robert-m-pirsig-on-the-book-he-wrote-and-the-one-he-didnt/).
- **QUA-A3 → QU-12, modular/integrated alternatives.** Compare constructing a
  representation then mapping with revising representation/mapping together.
  The research dispute justifies the test, not settled architecture.
  [Response](https://www.qrg.northwestern.edu/papers/Files/chalmers/response-Chalmers-et-al-JETAI.html).
- **QUA-A4 → QU-12, analogy breakpoints.** Preserve omissions/counterexamples
  beside correspondences; test near and distant transfers. A useful illustration
  does not authorize transfer to every family. Original extension of the later
  representation critique, not a GEB-specific claim.
  [Paper](https://cse.msu.edu/~cse841/papers/hofstader.pdf).
- **QUA-A5 → QUA-11/QU-12, Copycat limits.** The paper's system does not improve
  across runs, excludes much low-level perception and gives letters limited
  properties. Within-run restructuring is not durable learning or consciousness.
  [Paper](https://cse.msu.edu/~cse841/papers/hofstader.pdf).
- **QUA-A6 → QU-08, three causes of agreement.** Independently vary common
  evidence, shared framing and adoption rules. Hold two fixed while changing
  the third. Do not hardcode group proportions, demographics or personalities.
  [Interview](https://hbr.org/podcast/2024/10/malcolm-gladwells-new-take-on-tipping-points),
  [Watts–Dodds](https://pdodds.w3.uvm.edu/research/papers/others/2007/watts2007a.pdf).
- **QUA-A7 → QU-07, resource sweep.** Test several context/decomposition/
  coordination levels; two conditions cannot locate the failure boundary. No
  inverted-U resource law was verified. [Interview](https://knowledge.wharton.upenn.edu/article/david-goliath-malcolm-gladwell-advantages-disadvantages/).
- **QUA-A8 → QU-05, calibration.** Retain useful/misleading cues by task family
  and feedback history. Human-expertise grounding was abstract-level, and
  transfer to workers remains unproven. [Kahneman–Klein](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19739881/).

## Discovery-only reading and experiment leads

### QUA-14 — Track exception normalization

The _Blowup_ archival abstract names Diane Vaughan and normalization of deviance;
the full essay was not read.
[Archive](https://www.newyorker.com/magazine/1996/01/22/blowup-2).
Could individually authorized exceptions become invisible when no immediate
failure follows? Replay such a sequence; compare per-episode and cumulative
review of changed assumptions. This permits no exception to locked requirements.
Read the full source before attributing detailed mechanisms.

### QUA-15 — Test compositions of individually acceptable actions

The same abstract raises complex-system failure, not a complete DotLn causal
theory. [Archive](https://www.newyorker.com/magazine/1996/01/22/blowup-2).
Compose two supports that pass alone but share an artifact/prerequisite. Compare
composition-aware fixtures with isolated checks. Distinguish interaction failure
from blaming the actor that encounters it. Substantive source reading is pending.

### QUA-16 — Check behavioral adaptation after a safeguard

The abstract raises compensating behavior; underlying literature was not read.
[Archive](https://www.newyorker.com/magazine/1996/01/22/blowup-2).
Compare a safeguard with workload held fixed and after task/support choices
adapt. Observe larger tasks, reduced other checks and displaced supervision in
synthetic scenes. Do not infer that safeguards generally fail.

### QUA-17 — Judge a decision using the evidence available then

_Connecting the Dots_ appeared in anthology contents; its body was not read and
the record does not establish a hindsight theory.
[Contents](https://elibrary.point.edu/cgi-bin/koha/opac-detail.pl?biblionumber=7325).
Original experiment: review a stopped workstream without its later outcome,
then with it; check whether a proposed correction relies on unavailable future
information. Read the original essay before attributing the mechanism.

### QUA-18 — Preserve several useful preference profiles

_The Ketchup Conundrum_ was an unsampled preference lead.
[Contents](https://elibrary.point.edu/cgi-bin/koha/opac-detail.pl?biblionumber=7325).
Test presentations within tasks and across tasks before choosing a global
default; no presentation may win overall. Read the essay and underlying
preference research before treating this as its verified conclusion.

### QUA-19 — Compare observer predictions with later events

The university bibliography lists J. B. Marshall's 1999 Metacat dissertation;
Hofstadter was committee co-chair, not author. The dissertation was not read.
[Bibliography](https://cogs.indiana.edu/directory/faculty/profile.php?faculty=dughof).
An observer could retain predictions and compare later events to improve
intervention choices, rather than only narrate history. Inspect the work and
distinguish technical monitoring from subjective awareness claims.

### QUA-20 — Treat repeated errors as clues to representation

The university description lists error-making research and a named essay; the
work was not read. [Research description](https://cogs.indiana.edu/directory/faculty/profile.php?faculty=dughof).
Preserve recurring error structures and compare reframing the task with adding
one correction per symptom. Read the research before attribution; infer no
person/model psychology from this lead.

### QUA-21 — Preserve an organizing style across transformed examples

Letter Spirit dissertations and translation/analogy/constraint work appeared in
the bibliography but were not sampled.
[Bibliography](https://cogs.indiana.edu/directory/faculty/profile.php?faculty=dughof).
Given approved workflows, propose a new variant preserving conventions while
adapting to a task. Test fidelity, usefulness and constraints separately. Read
the underlying work; style cannot encode authority or correctness by assumption.

### QUA-22 — Compare selection from demos with selection from trials

_Most Likely to Succeed_, _The Talent Myth_ and _The New-Boy Network_ were
title-level leads, not inspected essays.
[Contents](https://elibrary.point.edu/cgi-bin/koha/opac-detail.pl?biblionumber=7325).
Choose patterns through task/recovery trials versus descriptions, fluency or a
polished demo. Read the essays and underlying research before attribution. This
concerns selection validity, separately from worker/scaffold effects.
