# Draw 001 — interactions that can be authored, tried and kept

Date: 2026-09-07. Executor synthesis; independent verification pending.
This atlas is a **lossy projection** of the [detailed research](research/README.md),
[source comparisons](research/source-comparison.md) and private reading records.
It becomes immutable at close. Ten cards select directions without deleting
unselected ideas. Discovery authorizes no implementation or policy activation.

Local references R-001–R-020 identify the declared pilot. Book/application IDs
identify the separate authorized research track; they do not increase local
yield. Current product interpretation and executable evidence govern adoption.
The strict local result is zero missing-meaning yields, with useful retained
intent, two historical tensions and substantial delivery opportunities.

## C-01 — Edit a behavior and see what changes

**Scene.** The operator gives one toy actor a delayed response with a stop
condition, authors several timed inputs and presses Run. They save the build,
change the delay and compare the first different decision. Next they apply the
supported policy to ten actors. A recorded software worker and simulated device
can later inhabit the same scene with their capabilities visible.

**Carried shape and lineage.** R-004 → R-016 → R-018 retains playful, progressive
authoring. R-019/R-020 retain temporal interpretation and bounded comparison.
AG-01/06/07/08 and IX-15/16/22/23/24 develop the application contract. These
are retained intent plus original experiments, not independent corroboration.
Homes: [RPG view](../../../../product/04-interfaces.md#rpg--path-of-exile-view),
[time fidelity](../../../../product/11-protino.md), and the planning map's
existing parity/drag-equip candidate.

**What is missing.** Current codecs describe equivalent loadouts; the compiled
diff compares declarations. The fixture-driven Seiri timeline is useful, but
it is not a general authored-input/virtual-time comparison driver. The new
contract must return executed paths and first divergence as well as configuration
delta. A static diagram alone does not satisfy this scene.

**Route / full candidate F-01.** Choose rung 3, a pure reactor over bounded
authored events, with rung 4 deterministic compilation/projection; reject a
rung 8 model prompt deciding every toy step. Consumer: pattern workshop and
`προτείνω`, reusing the compiler. Scope: one supported policy, then ten independent
actors. Activation: explicit preview command on pinned versions; expiry: run
budget, stop or cancellation. Authority effect: simulation only; real adapters
require their separately authorized capabilities. Success: two consumer entry
points agree and the operator can locate the first behavioral difference.
Failure: changed input or hidden model resampling masquerades as a policy effect.

**Lowering and return.** Felt control → edit/apply/run/compare → timed input and
response grammar → existing Cadence/Program/continuation mechanics → initial
state, input, clock, seed, capability and recorded-result contracts → a bounded
driver and UI application operation → an executable one-actor witness. A pure
reference model and pinned RxJS adapter are technical options to compare;
neither is selected here. WO-037 owns multi-active composition when required.

Returning to the scene changed this reading: a graph and changed cost label
would have looked complete while concealing the absence of executed comparison.
The needed payoff is an editable experience with a visible consequence. Lowering
loses open-ended improvisation and live uncertainty; it gains repeatable cases.
Label recorded replay and fresh sampling differently. Promotion requires the
full usable loop, meaningful conformance tests and independent evidence.

**Counterlens and residue.** Let a newcomer inspect and undo an edit without
understanding compiler internals; provide non-drag editing and readable timelines.
Do not animate requested movement as confirmed movement or infer private intent.
Compare declared costs, observed counts and measured costs separately. Spatial
coordination, arbitrary effects and unsupported operators remain later slices.

## C-02 — Grow a vocabulary for interaction at every scale

**Scene.** The same authoring surface describes a sensor remaining active, an
accepted commitment changing, a group waiting for contributions, a memory window
expiring and a proposed correction receiving later evidence. Patterns compose
into larger exchanges while their boundaries remain inspectable.

**Shape and lineage.** R-004 → R-016 and R-019 already carry richer composition
than message triggers. [IX-01–IX-24](research/interaction-algebra.md) preserve
the distinct original scenes; AG-06 and AGA-08 connect authored assets and a
pattern catalog. The [temporal interpretation candidate](../../../../product/05-pattern-library.md)
is the current home, not an exhaustive implemented vocabulary.

R-005/006 → R-017 also carries paired invitations and responses. The current
Marquet protocol preserves both sides while keeping actual authority distinct;
these images reinforce a relational grammar rather than a single autonomy dial.

**Route.** Choose rung 3 for explicit temporal state and rung 4 for reusable
composition; reject rung 9 universal prose for every interaction. Consumer:
pattern workshop and later concept batches. The four overlap policies in IX-01
are separate observable choices, not cosmetic names for one callback rule.

**Lowering and return.** Exchange → scoped event/time/state/effect contracts →
one discriminating scenario per pattern → lowering/conformance evidence →
authorable catalog item. Stop at unknown for unsupported joins and other
operators. Returning to the scene requires showing filtered, queued, superseded
and late events, not only successful output. Simplification must not hide work.

**Counterlens and residue.** RxJS offers a powerful expressive reference language;
expressibility still needs explicit effect, state and cancellation boundaries.
Unsubscription cannot undo an already performed external action. Preserve all
24 records, including memory, adaptation, authority and coordination, as the
continuing vocabulary rather than shrinking this to four overlap operators.

## C-03 — Find an idea, then follow it into actual use

**Scene.** A fresh planning session searches for “late reply,” finds the relevant
concepts and earlier decisions, and sees the next missing step. It selects ten
compatible concepts for a batch. Later the operator opens each delivered behavior
and follows its evidence back to the originating idea. The unselected ideas
remain findable for another available weekend.

**Shape and lineage.** This is the operator's explicit scope expansion. R-003/004
reinforce retained external memory and progressive growth; QU-03, QUA-04/05/10
and AGA-08 supply related experiments. The complete
[concept-pipeline proposal](idea-pipeline-proposal.md) is discoverable from the
[planning map](../../../../planning/work-order-map.md). It is not a local-source
yield or an adopted schema.

**Route / full candidate F-02.** Choose rung 4, deterministic text/metadata
retrieval and generated projections, with rung 7 detail on demand. Reject rung 9
loading the archive into every prompt and a rung 5 semantic model before a
retrieval miss justifies one. Consumer: planning now, application workshop later.
Scope: this research plus a small older-ledger sample and one app-use witness.
Activation: explicit capture, query or batch selection; expiry: a query ends,
while records persist with explicit supersession. Authority effect: retrieval
does not adopt, schedule or widen permissions.

**Lowering and return.** Remember and use → capture/find/relate/select/deliver/use
→ stable concept, links, next action and witness → existing source/product/order
identities → small versioned records plus query projection → retrieval and
delivery trial. Physical layout and exact schema remain a planning decision.
Success: an unfamiliar session retrieves concepts under alternate wording and
traverses source-to-use in both directions. Failure: manual status duplication,
whole-archive reading or a registry that grows without usable delivery.

Returning to the scene rules out metadata completeness as the goal. Capture
must stay cheap; planning detail arrives when needed. A batch means ten named
usable outcomes, with per-item evidence, or explicit unresolved items. Lowering
loses associative richness; detailed records and aliases preserve paths back.
Promotion requires the retrieval witness and one real consumer witness.

**Counterlens and residue.** Never let a summary replace distinct ideas. Preserve
splits, alternatives and rejected experiments. Ten is a useful batch size, not
permission to hide foundation work. No new work-order number family or lifecycle
is needed. Schema, first batch and exact orders await planning.

## C-04 — Turn a changing external artifact into useful work

**Scene.** An interchangeable enterprise-tracker connector imports a formatted
ticket artifact, its discussion and images. The operator checks its interpretation.
A later revision identifies which criteria and evidence need reconsideration.
Another connector can supply the same contract without importing deployment
assumptions from the first.

**Shape and lineage.** R-003 → related tracked-work ledger entries →
[Ports](../../../../product/03-architecture.md) already retains SourceAdapter →
SourceBundle → StoryContract, artifact-specific meaning and targeted revision
invalidation. R-015's short additions are not proof that this detail vanished.
IX-18 and AG-02 extend the scene. Private examples were generalized under the
operator's disposition; no private implementation is a design source.

**Route / full candidate F-03.** Choose rung 4 for normalized source bundles and
explicit revision links; use rung 5 only for genuinely ambiguous interpretation.
Reject rung 8 ad hoc prompt scraping with undocumented conventions. Consumer:
workstream intake/application, reusing the planned WO-034 source projection.
Scope: one original synthetic connector and rich artifact. Activation: import
or observed source revision; expiry: superseded source interpretation is retained
but no longer silently current. Authority effect: read/import does not grant
external write or messaging authority.

**Lowering and return.** Understand a living artifact → import/review/revise →
source revision and interpretation grammar → existing Ports contracts →
fixtures preserving formatting, image positions and evidence links → adapter
and consumer seed. Stop before selecting a vendor or live-account scope.
Success: changed criteria invalidate affected claims while unrelated evidence
remains attributable. Failure: OCR treated as truth, universal color semantics
or every source edit discarding all prior work.

Returning to the scene preserves the useful integration even though the
original example was private. Normalization can lose layout and discussion;
retain source-addressed observations and explicit uncertainty. Promotion needs
revision, duplicate, unavailable-source and interpretation-disagreement tests.

**Counterlens and residue.** Show imported scope and provenance to a newcomer.
Exclude private names/configuration/code from public fixtures. Two-way sync,
external question routing and real connectors remain distinct later slices.

## C-05 — Let an experienced correction help the next worker

**Scene.** The operator points to a premature completion. A candidate correction
keeps that failure and a nearby legitimate case. A fresh worker receives the
adopted scoped behavior, completes the case and exposes what governed it. Later
contradicting experience revises or retires the correction.

**Shape and lineage.** R-003/004 → R-015/016 retains scoped rules and progressive
learning. OF-02, QU-01/02, QUA-05/08 and IX-09/19 develop the continued-use trial.
The [feedback domain](../../../../product/02-domain-model.md) already has compiled
units and typed corrections. WO-039 owns ordinary-session harness lowering;
WO-040 owns initial migration; WO-041 records the remaining continuing route.

**Route.** Choose the cheapest rung sufficient for each correction, starting
with rung 1 tests/lints/scripts, rung 2 pre-effect guards or rung 4 deterministic
compilation before rung 5 evaluators. Reject default rung 9 global prose.
Consumer: existing feedback compiler and governed-session application.

**Lowering and return.** Felt wrongness → retained before/after examples → scoped
condition and response → existing FeedbackUnit/correction → regression plus
fresh-worker evidence → one continuing delivery slice. Returning to the scene
requires less repeated operator repair, not merely a smaller document. Keep
unformulated preferences as examples when reduction is not yet warranted.

**Counterlens and residue.** Count false activations and repair transferred to the
operator. Survival of a fault is not demonstrated learning. Existing feedback,
replay and recovery are assets to use; no duplicate engine is proposed. Keep
manual composition when it is valuable craft rather than automating it by default.

## C-06 — Author when a group has enough to proceed

**Scene.** Three contributors return evidence. One duplicates a reply, another
is late and a third never returns. The operator compares complete membership,
deadline, first result, qualified result with deadline fallback and event closure.
Each run shows why it closed and what happened to dissent and late evidence.

**Shape and lineage.** AG-04 and AGA-04a–e provide separately grounded policies;
IX-04 composes the scene. The [opinion-cohort candidate](../../../../product/03-architecture.md)
already specifies partial completion, seal and late-result concerns. Current
kernel All/Race are deferred. R-007/R-017 are reminders that diagram analogy
does not justify a universal primitive.

**Route.** Choose rung 3, an explicit correlated collection reactor; reject rung
8 model judgment of whether enough replies “feel” present. Consumer: temporal
workshop, then an actual cohort consumer. Scope and authority stay with that
consumer; a group label alone grants neither.

**Lowering and return.** Joint exchange → membership/correlation/completion/
combination → explicit closed outcome and late-result contract → bounded tests
→ authorable policies. Stop at a planning seed before selecting kernel types.
Returning to the scene preserves missing contributions and qualified dissent;
a single completed badge would erase both.

**Counterlens and residue.** Duplicates must not satisfy thresholds. Independent
workflows retain their settled independence. Ten actors do not need a global
barrier simply because they share a screen. Group strategy, formations and
ownership transfer remain different concepts with their own AGA/IX records.

## C-07 — Turn flow and feedback ideas into playable lessons

**Scene.** A queue grows while actors keep starting work. Two explanations
compete: limited downstream capacity or unusable proposals. Change one condition,
then introduce delayed feedback. The operator can compare accepted outcomes,
waiting, repeated intervention and work displaced elsewhere.

**Shape and lineage.** R-007/008/012 → R-017 preserves constraint and intervention
diagrams, but some historical interpretations overclaim universal kernel laws.
OF-01/03/06/07/08, MS-01–15 and GL-01–07 keep richer hypotheses and falsifiers.
The [capability/flow roadmap](../../../../product/06-roadmap.md) already retains
admission distinctions; current elapsed time does not measure queue waiting.

**Route.** Choose rung 3 for explicit toy dynamics and rung 5 for evaluating the
declared outcome; reject rung 6 universal scheduling barriers inferred from TOC.
Consumer: pattern workshop lessons. Any live admission policy requires separate
evidence and authority; this draw changes none.

**Lowering and return.** Frustrated flow → competing explanation → bounded stock,
arrival, completion and observation model → controlled input/delay scenario →
lesson and prediction trial. Returning to the scene must reveal a moved
constraint and the possibility that subtraction beats another intervention.
Small deterministic models expose assumptions; they do not prove social laws.

**Counterlens and residue.** Preserve affected-party costs, urgent exceptions and
conflicting goals. Do not equate worker utilization with useful throughput or
assign a permanent bottleneck identity to a person. Detailed systems methods
and unread chapters remain attributed leads, not ready-made product rules.

## C-08 — Keep work understandable through interruption and uncertainty

**Scene.** The operator returns after a worker vanished. The view shows the
current outcome, changed source, an unresolved effect, retained progress and the
next legal intervention. A replacement worker continues from recorded state.
The operator can inspect why a late result was retained but not applied.

**Shape and lineage.** R-001/002 → R-013/014 retains fresh orientation and durable
progress; ADR-0001 governs founding convergence. AG-02/03/05/08 and OF-04/05
provide use and comprehension trials. Existing continuation, fencing and receipts
already execute. [Workstream application](../../../../product/12-workstream-application.md)
and WO-033/034 own the consumer direction.

**Route.** Choose rung 4 projections of existing evidence, rung 2 acceptance
guards and existing rung 3 continuation; reject a rung 8 narrator inventing
progress or another universal process manager. Consumer: workstream shell.

**Lowering and return.** Return with confidence → changes/decision/evidence/next
action → existing records and invariants → interrupted/stale/uncertain-effect
fixtures → comprehension trial through the planned consumer. Returning to the
scene makes reconstruction effort a separate measurement from status latency.
Recorded explanations can omit human context; do not fabricate it to fill a gap.

**Counterlens and residue.** Show known, unresolved and attempted outcomes
distinctly. Respect accessibility and slower reading. Existing bounded dedup
does not establish exactly-once effects in arbitrary external systems. Wider
fault evidence belongs with WO-103/105, not a new recovery-engine proposal.

## C-09 — Preserve judgment and compare representations

**Scene.** Two behaviors satisfy the same formal conditions, but one feels clearer
to use. The operator marks that preference before writing a rule. Another scene
organizes the same idea as an actor practice, correction or interaction and
reveals different possibilities and losses.

**Shape and lineage.** R-009/011 → R-017 and current vision already treat
representations and operational approximation carefully. QU-02/03/04/12,
QUA-06/12/18/20/21 keep judgment, composition and representation distinct.
GEB text was not read; later author/research sources provide bounded leads.
The [vision](../../../../product/00-vision.md) does not claim exhaustive judgment.

**Route.** Choose rung 7 retained examples/reference, then rung 5 a provisional
evaluation when evidence warrants it; reject rung 9 a universal quality rule.
Consumer: comparison workshop and ideation/promotion.

**Lowering and return.** Immediate preference → marked example and context →
alternative representations → discriminating scenes → held-out comparison.
Stop at not-yet-reducible when a stable distinction has not emerged. Returning
to the scene protects quality of experience from being mistaken for a metric.
Formal lowering may erase ambiguity that was useful; retain the source links.

**Counterlens and residue.** Test order effects, reversals, capture effort and
actual reuse. A philosophical analogy establishes neither consciousness nor a
new kernel entity. Representation variation must yield a useful decision or
experiment, not just more creative wording. Deeper reading remains available.

## C-10 — Compare evidence, conditions and consequences

**Scene.** Six actors agree, but their citations converge on one mistaken
summary. Another run changes only available observations; a third changes the
policy. The operator can tell these interventions apart and include repair
cost when deciding whether an apparent improvement helped.

**Shape and lineage.** R-010 is unendorsed generated image text, not scientific
confirmation; R-019/020 already distinguish observations, interpretations and
time. AG-07, QU-05–11, QUA-07/10/13/17/19 and IX-05/11/21/23 preserve different
experiments. [Bounded comparison](../../../../product/11-protino.md) is their
current home; these research sources are not independent evidence of efficacy.

**Route.** Choose rung 4 input/provenance projections plus rung 5 controlled
evaluation; reject rung 8 fluent consensus as truth. Consumer: `προτείνω`,
workshop and later delivery evaluation.

**Lowering and return.** Convincing outcome → source and condition separation →
shared fixture with one changed variable → observation/policy/path evidence →
held-out trial. Stop at inconclusive when inputs or fresh model sampling confound
comparison. Returning to the scene requires showing what each actor could know,
not a god's-eye explanation attributed to them.

**Counterlens and residue.** Shared citations are not multiple votes. Avoid
mind-reading, identity judgments, universal practice thresholds or automatic
popularity-based adoption. Technical success and intended benefit are separate
claims. Preserve unsuccessful and uncertain trials with the successful ones.
