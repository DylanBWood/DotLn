# Proposed work-order sequence

The operator-authored sequence below is the single editable input to the index
and refutation subject. It grants no activation authority. For rationale see
[the planning map](work-order-map.md). Closed entries leave the sequence at the next
planning pass (operator direction, 2026-09-22); the generated work-order
index's Closed section is their record, and the map and archive keep their
rationale. A withdrawn entry (WO-158) is settled the same way and listed in
that section under its disposition.

Planning entry returns the pending [follow-up register](followups.json) in
bounded pages. Use `npm run plan -- followups` for its current counts, source
pointers and continuation command; untouched items persist across passes.

A pass's dated note leaves with the last entry it places, as a closed entry
does (2026-09-30 pass): its planning document, the map and the ledger keep
it. The lane-pair rule (operator direction, 2026-09-16; recut 2026-09-17) is
in [the map](work-order-map.md#tracks-and-dispatch-dimensions): entries are
grouped two per blank-separated pair for two parallel lanes, with disjoint
primary surfaces and no hard edge inside a pair, one delivery order and by
preference one evidence-only or machinery order; one order at a time passes
final review and release close.

Standing placements from earlier passes: the run from WO-061 keeps its
serial reading order until R2 (2026-09-16). WO-072 depends on WO-123, which
carries the source-change guard fix (2026-09-28). WO-088 and WO-089 stay
last under their 2026-09-19 holds, and the pass that reaches WO-088
re-observes its gap (2026-09-28).

Standard pass (2026-09-30, second): the operator's items, WO-172's subject map
and a product coverage check filed eight orders. Seven have closed; WO-183
(intent declaration and the stranger test, the `v1.0.0` exit) remains
after WO-118 because the operator puts that exit months away. The period
below `v1.0.0` is early access (operator direction, 2026-09-30): planning
drains this queue and the operator's direction sets the rungs after it.
The closed placements remain in [the planning document](standard-pass-2026-09-30.md)
§7 to §10 and the map.
