## F10 — A failed suite's fixture past its diagnosis window is never pruned

**Severity:** high. **Route:** blocking (criterion 3; criterion 15 by consequence). **Class:** a failed gate fixture past its diagnosis window that no lifecycle removes, because the prune's judgment of a nested root that shares its lifetime never concludes. **Repair rule:** a removed repository is observed absence. No gate run, lane or writer can live in it, so the judge proceeds to owner liveness and retention instead of retaining on the error. That applies to the containing fixture's repository and to the nested root's own, at release close, at the operator's prune and at gate end alike. A nested root that shares an ended fixture's recorded lifetime ends with it, whatever its class protocol (a warm cache key, or a coordination root recorded inside the fixture). Live owners, live lanes and gates, live writers, null retention and invalid records still protect, and the judge still retains on an observation that is genuinely unavailable. The repair closes the class, not one call site. A regression case proves, through `pruneHarness` with no finishing gate, that such a fixture is a candidate in three cases: after its worktree is removed, after the nested root's repository is removed, and when the nested root is a warm fixture. The 47 roots this worktree's failed gates left are removed by this order's release-close prune once their windows pass, and D053's eligibility claim for `3sHpVT` is corrected. Recorded as [D057](../../evidence/WO-233/decisions.md#wo-233-d057--ver-004-f10-a-failed-suites-fixture-past-its-diagnosis-window-is-never-pruned) (FUP-cf88f38430ab7ec5).

There are two causes:

- **The ENOENT.** For any nested root with a `fixtureOwner`, `blockedByUse` calls `activeGateRuns` on the containing fixture's repository. It also calls it on the nested root's own repository, unless the gate finishing is the fixture's. `activeGateRuns` resolves the path through `realpathSync`, which throws once the directory is gone. `judgeTemporaryRoot` retains on any error, so the nested root and its ancestors stay retained.
- **The warm fixture.** The warm-fixture branch retains a current or unavailable key before it checks any containing lifetime.

Release close removes the subject worktree in its cleanup rows (`scripts/release.mjs` 2323 to 2462) and then calls `pruneHarness` (2502 to 2505). That worktree is the owner repository of every fixture its gates left. `pruneContext` passes no finishing gate. So the close's dry run, with the worktree present, lists such a fixture as a candidate, and the close itself retains it. Every later prune retains it too.

The defect predates the repair. VER-001's subject (checkpoint 3) already called `activeGateRuns(record.owner.repository)` unguarded. The VER-001 repair guarded only the completing gate's case and added the containing-fixture call, and no earlier verification pruned after a worktree removal.
