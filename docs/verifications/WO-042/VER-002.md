## VER-001 finding F1 — resolved

**Prior severity:** moderate. **Current disposition:** resolved; criterion 5 is
met.

**Expected.** A contradictory authored note that names an exact
opposite-envelope effect id must reject with `INSPECTION CONTRADICTION`.
Whitespace, prose punctuation, backticks, and angle brackets delimit tokens;
dots and colons extend ids internally, with a trailing run acting as sentence
punctuation only before another boundary or the end of the note.

**Observed.** `inspectionEffectPattern` now escapes the entire literal effect
id, applies one Unicode-aware boundary on both sides, and handles trailing dots
and colons without splitting punctuation inside the id. The ten repair fixtures
cover em dash, en dash, ellipsis, curly double quote, and curly single quote in
both authored-note directions. A second fixture covers literal ids containing
dots, colons, underscores, plus signs, and parentheses, followed by six classes
of longer-token negatives in both directions.

**Independent reproduction.** After a fresh build, I compiled fixture-derived
supports directly through `packages/compiler/dist`, without calling
`authority-evidence.mjs`. Restrictions containing `repo.inspect` followed by
em dash, en dash, ellipsis, or curly quotes, and grants containing denied
`repo.delete` beside em dash or curly quotes, all returned `ok: false` with
`INSPECTION CONTRADICTION`. `repo.inspectExtra`, `prefixrepo.inspect`,
`repo.inspect.child`, and `repo.inspect:detail` all returned `ok: true` with no
diagnostic. The focused AC5/F1 run passed 14/14 and the complete authority file
passed 38/38.

**Impact after repair.** The documented authored-note consistency check now
covers the punctuation class that caused the failure without widening or
narrowing executable authority. The guard still compares explicit literal ids;
it does not claim natural-language inference.
