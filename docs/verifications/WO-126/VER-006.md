and `executableInvocations` (`:562-566`) applies `refuseDeniedProgram` to
programs outside `dataPrograms` and `effectPrograms`. So every operand after
an effect program's subcommand is data, although git's `rebase --exec`,
`bisect run`, `submodule foreach`, `difftool -x|--extcmd`, `filter-branch`'s
filters and `config <executed key> <value>`, npm's `explore -- <command>`,
Yarn's `workspace <name> exec` and gh's `alias set --shell` execute their
values. VER-005's repair direction said "commit messages, `--grep` patterns
and the other subcommand operands stay data"; the repair followed it, and
the direction did not enumerate the operands these programs execute.

**Consequence.** As F24: `remote.unapproved` and `package.publish` are the
effects the no-commits-before-final-review rule depends on, and the Codex
profile has no other layer. D020's reopening condition is met for the sixth

configuration route and `npm explore` and `yarn workspace … exec` are
execution wrappers of the same programs the repair recursed into.
