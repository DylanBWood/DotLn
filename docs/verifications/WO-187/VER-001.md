## Findings

Every finding below lies in the order's declared surfaces and takes the `blocking` route. D019 holds the rule each repair must hold.

**F1 (criterion 2, blocking):** the verify briefing loses `Known issues and carry-ins` sections in real and common layouts.
- **Where:** `scripts/lib/verification-briefing.mjs:19` starts a section only at a label alone on its line. Line 25 ends it at any bold-led line.
- **Observed:**
  - `docs/work-orders/WO-123-vertical-composition.md:466` opens `**Known issues and carry-ins:** every duty this order owes…` followed by eight bullets. The briefing prints only the receipt block, with no advisory.
  - The layouts `**Known issues and carry-ins**:` and `## Known issues and carry-ins:` print nothing.
  - A section whose first paragraph is bold-led (`**Receipt 038:** …`) prints nothing.
  - A second copy of the section is dropped.
- **Expected:** the order's section is printed, as for the fixture layout.
- **Reproduction:** the executor's lifecycle fixture, copied to scratch with only the label changed to carry same-line text, fails with `AssertionError: verify must print the order's known issues`. `verificationKnownIssues` over all 193 orders prints 14 of the 15 sections; WO-123 is the one missed.
- **Severity:** medium. Of the open orders, only WO-123 currently uses that layout, but nothing stops a planner writing it, and the omission is silent.
- **Repair rule:** D019 F1.

**F2 (criterion 4, blocking):** finding lines the reader does not recognise vanish from the count.
- **Where:** `scripts/lib/review-findings.mjs:34` accepts an F-number only when `:` or a dash follows it. `markdownLines` (line 11) treats a line opening with inline triple-backtick code as a fence.
- **Observed** (`verdict: fail`, all counts zero, no advisory):
  - `**F2. Stale version label.**` (WO-100 FINAL-001's shape)
  - `- **F1, major, criterion 6: …**` (WO-164 FINAL-001's shape)
  - `1. **Finding F1:** blocking; class: escape; …`
  - `**Finding F1 (blocking):** …`
  - `` ```npm test``` passed `` followed by two canonical finding lines
- **Recap lines:** `- F6–F8 and F10 stay repaired…` and `- F2: VER-001's finding was repaired.` count as two unclassed findings.
- **Expected:** a failed review never records as a measured zero without an advisory, and the escape measure never loses a finding silently.
- **Reproduction:** the variant probe over `reviewFindings` in session scratch. The adversary also found WO-041 and WO-178 failed reviews that parse to zero findings.
- **Severity:** medium. The new reviewer text asks for the canonical form, but the corpus shows reviewers vary their forms. The measure this order exists to create would undercount silently from its first use.
- **Repair rule:** D019 F2.
