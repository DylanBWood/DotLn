## Finding requiring repair

### N1 — major: the generated role text breaches two cold-start ceilings, which are left advisory

- **Criterion.** The standing operator direction in product 07, lines
  1532–1541: "a reviewed rule that breaches a ceiling raises it in the same
  change by one 4 KB step above the measured bytes with the rule named, or
  records a dated acceptance for that metric; the breach is never trimmed
  around, never resolved by cutting another rule, and never left advisory
  across orders." The same direction is recorded in the `source` of
  `docs/control/budgets.json`. It is also relevant to AC6, the regenerated
  bundle.
- **Observed.** WO-054 appends one 194-byte sentence to the shared generated
  role text. The sentence begins "Other tool and completion judgments are
  advisory … The separate Codex compaction adapter restores…". It lands in
  `CLAUDE.md` and in every `.claude/skills` and `.agents/skills` role file.
  - Measured as installed `CLAUDE.md` plus the role skill, the tool's method:
    - executor goes from 5,280 + 15,181 = 20,461 at `HEAD` to 5,474 + 15,375
      = 20,849, against a ceiling of 20,480;
    - release-close goes from 5,280 + 6,769 = 12,049 to 5,474 + 6,963 =
      12,437, against a ceiling of 12,288.
  - Both were within their ceilings at `main`, and both are breached here.
  - `docs/control/budgets.json` is unchanged from `HEAD`: no raised limit and
    no dated acceptance for either metric.
  - `harness-context --check` and `meta --check` report the breach only as an
    advisory ("planning input") and exit 0, so no gate catches it.
  - The executor's decisions and repair record do not mention it.
- **Expected.** In this change, for each of `coldStartBytes.executor` and
  `coldStartBytes.release-close`, either raise the ceiling under the
  documented route with the rule named, or record a dated acceptance. The
  precedent is the 2026-09-18 WO-139 verifier acceptance: measured bytes, the
  rule named, and one 4 KB step. Do not trim the sentence or another rule to
  fit. A re-run of `harness-context --check` should then show no breach
  advisory for these two metrics.
- **Reproduction.** Run `node scripts/harness-context.mjs --check`; the first
  line is the advisory. `git diff HEAD -- docs/control/budgets.json` is empty.
  `git show HEAD:CLAUDE.md | wc -c` gives 5,280;
  `git show HEAD:.claude/skills/dotln-executor/SKILL.md | wc -c` gives
  15,181.
- **Likely surface.** `docs/control/budgets.json` (`limits.coldStartBytes`
  and/or `acceptances`), with a decision entry in
  `docs/evidence/WO-054/decisions.md`. The runtime behavior needs no change.
- **Severity rationale.** This is not a product-behavior defect. But the
  documented route requires it to be settled in the same change. Merging as
  is would carry the breach advisory into the next order, which is exactly
  what the direction forbids. The repair is one small edit to the budgets
  file. The operator may also choose which route to record.
