**Severity:** blocking for criterion 7; medium impact on bounded startup and
close latency. **Class:** escape, present in the verified subject and in the

[filed executable probe](../../evidence/WO-253/VER-001-budget-probe.mjs)

probe checks that the clock really expired before asserting the result.

**Observed:** the corrected run at 2026-11-04T09:47:45.787Z reports
`clockAtRemoval: 2` under `budgetMs: 1`, `checksAfterExpiry: 0`,
`deletedAfterExpiry: 1`, `snapshotExists: false`, `remaining: 0` and
`stoppedAt: null`. It exits 1 at the partial-stop assertion.

**Expected:** expiry during the permission/deletion walk stops the apply,
retains a resumable remainder as `budget`, and records the stop and remaining
count. The guides expressly promise this, and order step 5 requires it.

**Cause and repair rule:** `applyPlannedCandidate` in
`scripts/lib/harness-prune.mjs` rechecks the candidate with the budget, then
calls `makeTemporaryWritable` and recursive `rmSync` without it.
`makeTemporaryWritable` in `packages/skeleton/src/temporary-root-records.mjs`
also recurses without a budget. Carry the budget through those later walks
and deletion, preserve the existing proof and confinement checks, and return
an accurate resumable partial result. Add coverage that expires after the
proof recheck. Checking only before the candidate cannot satisfy this rule.

**Limits:** this is a deterministic missing-check reproduction, not a measured
wall-clock overrun on a large live root. It exercises the documented clock
seam and an ordinary supported snapshot. It is separate from D022's noted
publication-listing timeout. No implementation change was made to influence
the result.
