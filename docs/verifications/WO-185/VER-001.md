Criterion 3; also the Objective ("no surviving descendant") and the Design ("sweeps for surviving task processes when the gate ends").

- **Observed.**

  - The repair names the inheritable mark of ownership it relies on and the cases that mark still cannot see, such as a daemon that closes its descriptors and clears its environment. It records those cases as limits rather than claiming absolute containment.
