All are low severity and in the order's declared surfaces; none was reproduced.

1. **Duplicate guard incidents.** The guard marks only `killedProcesses` as handled. A later candidate sharing a protected or already-gone member can therefore draw a second incident. Its stopId is normally deduplicated, but a member exiting between the census and the kill can over-count one stop.
2. **Signal after a resource stop.** A signal after the runner's own gate stop is classified as an interrupt when the host latest-incidents write has failed, so no row is recorded. The kill has already happened.
3. **One lease lookup per nested gate.** The gate-entry lease lookup predates the repair. The repair now passes its result to every acquire, so a transient failure at entry affects the whole nested gate. Overall exposure is lower than one lookup per task.
4. **Lost denial detail.** `finish()` and `stopTasks()` no longer attach a denied-signal detail to the row.
5. **Mislabelled cleanup failure.** A registration-withdrawal failure is labelled `launch`.

D028 records the rule each repair must hold and the reopen conditions.

- Live-guard upgrade (FUP-c1a89d52cc314af9).
