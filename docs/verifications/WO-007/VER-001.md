## Findings

### F1 - Blocking - Planning drafts reproduce local intake too closely

The clean-room rule says `docs/intake/` is raw local material and must be
synthesized, not copied verbatim into committed files. A summary-only 10-gram
comparison over the nine 2026-09-02 local intake notes and the committed planning
surfaces found large exact overlaps:

```text
WO-018 draft: 680 shared unique 10-grams out of 702 (96.9%)
WO-016 draft: 741 / 811 (91.4%)
WO-019 draft: 930 / 1029 (90.4%)
WO-022 draft: 726 / 1016 (71.5%)
WO-017 draft: 657 / 946 (69.5%)
WO-020 draft: 804 / 1321 (60.9%)
WO-021 draft: 547 / 977 (56.0%)
```

A separate exact nonblank-line comparison between the cited local intake notes
and their corresponding work-order drafts found:

```text
docs/work-orders/WO-016-skeleton-reactor.md: 105/113
docs/work-orders/WO-017-kernel-truthfulness.md: 86/122
docs/work-orders/WO-018-control-plane-consolidation.md: 99/101

docs/work-orders/WO-021-control-plane-beacons.md: 71/122

```

The scan did not print or preserve raw intake text. The issue is not a detected
secret, employer identifier, or internal URL; targeted scans found no obvious
credential or internal-host leak in the changed public surfaces. The issue is
provenance: the overlap is too high to satisfy the repository's
"synthesize and rewrite" boundary without an explicit explanation that the
intake files were generated from the committed drafts rather than the other way
around. Repair must either rewrite the affected drafts from the operator intent
or add a durable, reviewable provenance explanation that makes the direction of
copying and clean-room safety unambiguous.

### F2 - Blocking - The 2026-09-02 intake batch is not single-copy in the main checkout

`CLAUDE.md` says `docs/intake` is gitignored single-copy, and WO-109's own
clean-room gate says the intake must be located in the main checkout and read in
place, never copied into a worktree. The read-only file listing taken during
verification shows the new 2026-09-02 planning/re-mining intake notes are present
under this WO-007 worktree's ignored `docs/intake/notes`, while the main checkout
has only the beacon ideation note from that 2026-09-02 set. Because those notes
are ignored raw material, keeping the active batch only in the worktree creates a
provenance and recoverability ambiguity before final review.

This finding does not authorize copying, deleting, or rewriting raw intake as
part of verification. It means repair must reconcile the single-copy raw intake
location and record the safe provenance state before another final-review attempt.

Evidence: `CLAUDE.md:63-65`;
`docs/work-orders/WO-109-shape-first-source-remine.md:120-128`; read-only
listings of `/Users/dylanwood/Projects/DotLn/docs/intake/notes` and
`/Users/dylanwood/Projects/DotLn-wo007/docs/intake/notes`.
