## Limits of this verification

- I judged the recorded episodes; I did not relaunch them. Re-running live
  vendor episodes is outside the authority this dispatch holds, and the order's
  own evidence gate is the receipts plus `npm test`.
- The proof establishes a **bounded observed effect** — protected checkout and
  sentinel byte equality before and after — not universal filesystem
  confinement and not orphan-worker fencing. The kill lands after the worker has
  exited. The receipt, `implementation.md` and the capability-table row all say
  this in terms; I confirm the narrower claim is the one actually made.
- `parentHandoff` measures the evidence collector's child-stdout sink, which is
  the fixture's own handoff boundary, not the orchestrating executor
  conversation. Growth of the complete executor conversation is `unknown` in
  every receipt, and the order's execution readback admits exactly this reading.
  The envelope-only result should not be cited as whole-session containment.
- The Codex inspection authorization is prompt-level text in
  `writerPrompt.inspectionInstructions`; enforcement remains the compiled guard
  and host permissions. The receipt states this. It is a real limit, disclosed,
  not a defect.
- Launch identity for every episode stays a launch claim with `unknown` effective
  readback, so the harness versions, models and efforts in the receipt table are
  what was requested, not what was measured. Criterion 3 requires this, and the
  cost figures are transport-reported episode counters for successful episodes
  only; the six failed attempts add unrecorded cost and no efficiency claim is
  made.
