## Not verified

- Native model verification. `MountedBeaconVerifier` is deterministic, and the
  evidence, the ledger, and 03 all say so; WO-010 remains the open work.
- Concealment from a hostile same-user process. The order records this as
  impossible here, and the implementation claims only a finite host input
  projection. I did not attempt to disprove a claim nobody makes.
- Cryptographic authorship or enumeration resistance. The 16-bit residue is
  documented as a weak keyed consistency and error-detection signal, and every
  surface I read repeats that limit rather than overselling it.
- Behavior on a dense (non-sparse) filesystem with a key configured. The design
  refuses rather than falling back, which I confirmed by feeding
  `writeV3BeaconFile` a `sparse: false` storage record; I did not obtain a
  genuinely dense volume to observe the end-to-end lifecycle warning.
