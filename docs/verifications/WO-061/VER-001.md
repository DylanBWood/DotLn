## Findings

**F1 — structural-rule coverage gap outside the declared fixtures (boarded; low severity; not a criterion failure).**
- **Observed:** `compileStoryContract` records `follows` only when the entry immediately after a question has another role. With entries e-q (reporter, a question), e-more (reporter) and e-reply (reviewer), the relations list is `[]`.
- **Expected:** the Design says "the next entry by another role is recorded only as the structural fact `follows`." Inference: this most naturally names e-reply, the first later entry whose role differs.
- **Why the fixtures miss it:** in every pinned fixture, the entry right after a question has another role, so both readings give the same facts.
- **Reproduction:** compile that three-entry discussion against `packages/compiler/dist` at the judged identity.
- **Rule a repair must hold:** position may create only `follows`, never `answers`. The chosen reading must be stated beside `STORY_RULE_PATTERNS` and pinned by a fixture of question, same-role follow-up, then a reply by another role.
- **Recorded in:** D010, with its named follow-up.

**Observation (not a finding).** A supplied class entry may use a class that a rule also emits, such as `struck` over unmarked text. It is recorded with origin `inferred` and no `rule`, so a consumer can still tell markup-decided facts from supplied ones. The order forbids changing a rule-decided span, and the overlap refusal enforces that.
