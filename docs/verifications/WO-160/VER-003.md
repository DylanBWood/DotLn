Weighed against the traps:
- **Rule beating, drift to low performance and seeking the wrong goal:** the judgment rests on retained-entry reachability after real Git exits, compared with Git's own drop. It does not rest on removal counts or on the executor's fixtures alone.
- **Success to the successful:** the same probes against the pre-repair code show they can fail.
- **Shifting the burden to the intervenor:** a pass removes the manual ref rescue that F5 would have left to the operator.
- **Escalation, tragedy of the commons and policy resistance:** boarding O29 beside O20 keeps the repair bounded and keeps the lock and sequential behavior that already matches Git.
