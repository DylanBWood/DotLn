## F1 — Amendments the previous release bound over the field stop matching

**Severity:** high. **Route:** blocking (a reproduced regression of behavior `main` had). **Class:** an admission implemented as an unconditional reinterpretation of bound bytes. **Repair rule:** the amendment match must admit every binding the previous release's reader admitted; the leading-field normalization may only widen what matches. Changed criterion, objective, dependency and Cost bytes after binding still refuse under both readings. A plan-refutation case proves that a binding computed by the base reader over the field and a mid-text change passes the plan gate.

That call deletes a leading `**Workstream:** WS-NNN` line whenever the filed source lacked one (`scripts/lib/plan-continuation.mjs` 308 to 332). Before this change, `amendPlanOrder` bound the current source with the line kept. That held from v0.96.0, which introduced the field, through v0.97.1; the reader is the same blob, `bdfe1919`, at v0.96.0, v0.97.1 and the activation base. So a binding recorded after an operator added the field together with any non-append change includes the line. The subject's `current` form drops the line and misses the bound prefix. Its `legacy` form then requires that form to begin with the filed source, which a mid-text change breaks.
