## Findings for repair

### F1 — criterion 2: `plan check` is still above 2 s

**Severity:** blocking for the stated acceptance criterion. The product impact is minor: about 0.7 s above the bound on each check, after a measured 84% reduction.

**Expected:** `node scripts/refute-plan.mjs check` finishes in under 2 s of wall-clock time on the operator's host. The order's objective says the same.

**Observed:** 2.75 s, 2.69 s and 2.68 s, all exit 0. The executor recorded 2.85 s and did not claim the bound (D003).

**Reproduce:** run `/usr/bin/time -p node scripts/refute-plan.mjs check` in the worktree.

**Limits:** On the current order text, no repair can reach the bound within scope. This is an inference from the profile above. Synchronous Git spawning is the largest remaining cost, and the order lists batching or caching Git reads as a non-goal. The design also says to touch nothing else in the builder. [D003](../../evidence/WO-156/decisions.md#wo-156-d003) files that cost as `FUP-3dc0266d6b87b939`. [D004](../../evidence/WO-156/decisions.md#wo-156-d004) records this finding, and its follow-up (`FUP-5827640154a48b7b`) names the two operator routes. The first is a scope expansion that admits the Git-spawn reduction, bound with `plan amend-order`. The second is an operator-authorized amendment of criterion 2. Choosing between them belongs to the operator, not to this verifier. Removing most of the 1.32 s of `spawnSync` self time would plausibly bring the check under 2 s. That estimate is unmeasured.
