## F1 — zsh clobber-override redirects record a `!`-prefixed destination, so live-gate writes into `packages/*/dist` and `./node_modules` are admitted

**Severity: blocking. Criterion 9, second kept refusal.**

Criterion 9 keeps exactly two refusals. The second is "a write to gate inputs or the success record during the reviewer's live `npm test`". Its fixture requires that "a live-gate write [is] still refused".

Mechanism, at the current source:

1. The tokenizer keeps `!` as an ordinary word character (`packages/skeleton/src/harness-command.ts:252–253`). `>!path` is therefore one word, and `>&!path` is one word after the duplication branch at :203–211.
2. The ampersand-redirect pattern at :323 (`/^\d*(>>?)&(.*)$/`) and the plain-redirect pattern at :339 (`/^(?:\d*>>?|&>>?)(.*)$/`) both capture everything after the operator. The destination recorded is `!packages/skeleton/dist/src/harness-command.js`.
3. `gateInputPath` resolves that literal spelling. Its first segment `!packages` escapes the dist and installed-root rules, while `git check-ignore` still matches the unanchored `dist/` (`.gitignore:10`) and `node_modules/` (`.gitignore:9`) components. The spelling is judged scratch, not an input.
4. The guard's `paths.every(!gateInputPath)` branch (`packages/skeleton/src/harness-host.ts:2289–2300`) returns no refusal. zsh then writes the real file.
