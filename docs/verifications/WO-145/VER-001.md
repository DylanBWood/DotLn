## Further limits recorded

1. **The first trial cannot feed the pre-registered reading on its own.**
   WO-145-D002 carries `effect.wallSecondsPerOrder: null` and
   `effect.tokensPerOrder: null`; its measured saving is 58.74 s per eligible
   development iteration, with frequency unknown. The reading in WO-145-D001
   requires a saving over the next ten orders exceeding total trial cost, which
   no null-valued record can supply. This is the honest state rather than an
   error — the generator is right to refuse invented zeros — but if the two
   later trials also record null per-order effects, the pre-registered reading
   will be uncomputable and the support will default to staying optional. Both
   D001 and D002 carry reopening conditions that reach this, so it is boarded
   up and needs no repair now.
2. **The recorded token cost is an upper bound, not an allocation.**
   `cost.tokens` 497,138 is the transcript counter across the measurement
   window, including contemporaneous source reads. The executor says so in
   WO-145-D003. Any later total that sums these figures is summing upper
   bounds.
3. **A decline that runs nothing cannot be recorded.** `checkExperiment`
   requires non-empty `cost.commands` and `effect.commands` even when
   `execution` is `"declined"`. This follows the order's own Design text, which
   asks every experiment record to carry measured cost and effect "with the
   commands that show both", so it is faithful rather than defective; an
   executor who declines after any investigation can name what they ran. It is
   an edge the later trials may meet, and it is worth one sentence in whichever
   pass revisits the contract.
