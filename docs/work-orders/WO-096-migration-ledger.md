**Design (scope discipline):**

- The rules are stated from WO-040 as they now hold; nothing is copied from
  any predecessor file.
- **Rows.** One row per shape: a generic `shapeId`; its category
  (`invariant`, `reactor`, `evaluator`, `transformer`, `workflow`,
  `knowledge`, `preference`, `incident`, `obsolete`); the cheapest
  sufficient rung (02 §Feedback); its status (`compiled` with the unit id,
  `batch-N`, `reference` for knowledge that stays an on-demand reference, or
  `declined` with a reason); its derived governance mode; and a one-line
  synthesized note citing the idea ledger, never intake. A shape an
  existing check enforces (WO-063's outward-artifact lint, for one) is
  `compiled` against that order, and the rule reads that check as its unit.
- **Exclusions.** A shape describing an employer's tracker, environment,
  login, team or project gets no row, only a count per exclusion class
  (`excluded.employer-specific: N`) with no name, reason or paraphrase; the
  operator's classification stays in ignored intake. A shape is generalized
  to a public class only where a generic shape exists with no employer
  detail, and its row says only that.
- **Denominator.** The executor reads every shape of the capture to
  classify it and counts them; where a dated ledger section names the
  capture, the rows pin its SHA-256, the receipt discloses the count and
  rows plus exclusion counts equal it. No public capture names a SHA-256
  today, so `capture` records `null` and `"unknown"` with the ten-rule table
  as the public source (the 2026-10-07 pass; reopen: such a capture appears
  in the ledger).
- **Governance mode is derived by one rule.** A `reference` or `declined`
  row is `none`. A row is `mechanism` only when its unit is equipped in the
  Contributor build, is neither `mechanism.kind: prose` nor a rung-seven
  role-skill unit (no host predicate), and no hand-written sentence
  restating it remains in the always-on set; every other row is `prose`.
  The always-on set is what a role loads before acting: the instruction
  file outside its generated block, the role procedure and support text the
  role skills carry, and the product 07 sections their `Read` directives
  name (`directedReads`). A unit's generated role-skill line and residue
  lines are its lowering, not a restatement. `mechanism` claims an
  executable handler, not a refusal: the hard enforcement stays the five
  refusals (02 §Feedback compiler v1). Where a profile's residue carries a
  `mechanism` row's unit as role text (Codex, for the six hook-lowered
  units), the render names that profile and counts per profile.
- **Render and check.** The render gives counts per status and governance
  mode beside the two points above, resolves the `lineage` root through
  `scripts/lib/config.mjs` and reads the verifier's typed restatement
  findings. `--check` refuses a stale render, an equipped unit missing from
  the rows, a `mechanism` row whose hash-pinned retired sentence is present
  again, and a retirement with no covering unit; its local-terms pass over
  every row id, note, unit id, incident summary and behavior sentence
  refuses a match, reports `unavailable` (never a pass) when the list is
  absent, and refuses an empty or malformed list; nothing about the list is
  committed or hashed. It is a document suite with a `.gitattributes` row
  and a document-gate stub; a suite that cannot run in an export without
  `corpus/feedback/` is declared `kit: "core-only: …"` (WO-201's rule).
- **Selection.** Twelve new units such that every category that can lower
  to a mechanism has a member across the ten and the twelve; at least eight
  at rung 1 or 2; at least one role-skill unit at rung 7; at least one
  cadence-shaped (a wait, retry or stall threshold as a kernel `Cadence`,

  never `mechanism`. The ten-rule table is the starting point; the executor
