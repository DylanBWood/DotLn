**Design (scope discipline):**

- The rule is enforced by comparing with the captures, not by reading
  prose. Rejected: a classifier of quotation marks alone (the axis is what
  the screen can know: a verbatim copy without quotation marks is the
  instance's observed case, and the ignored captures are the one source of
  the operator's bytes a check can hold against committed text without
  printing them). The screen reads the captures locally and prints a file,
  a line and a digest, never the words; without captures it says so and
  passes. The window is the check's declared constant, recorded in the
  decisions with its reason.
- The exception is typed, not prose. Rejected: a paragraph saying the
  operator asked (the axis is the reviewer's check: `operatorQuote` already
  carries `captureSha256`; `requestedIn` names the capture of the
  operator's request, and the verifier reads both).
- Forward-only. Rejected: screening the whole tree (the axis is the
  record: historical records keep their bytes; `doc-baseline.json` already
  splits current from historical, and the verbatim screen reads the files
  a branch changed against the merge base with `main`).
- The hand-back is journaled under the host's source. Rejected: dropping
  the row (the axis is the record: the journal keeps every prompt it saw,
  and a hand-back is a prompt the host wrote).
- The terms task screens what changed. Rejected: every committed file on
  every gate (the axis is cost: the export already screens its whole kit
  once; a gate screens the branch's change).
- The footer names a commit that holds the destinations because the
  records are committed before they move. Rejected: pushing each
  checkpoint as a ref (the axis is what a reader reaches: a checkpoint is a
  recovery object outside the branch's history, while the order branch's
  history is pushed with the launchpad records publish and stays reachable
  through the merge commit). Rejected: the kit guide saying the footer
  gives the layout only (the axis is the fork's check: every relative link
  resolving through GitHub alone).
