**Design (scope discipline):**

- Rewrite, never shorten for its own sake: each section of the guide is
  rewritten in place in this shape: a sentence or two on what the section
  is for and who needs it; numbered steps for a procedure; a list for rules;
  a list or table for flags and options; command blocks unchanged; the
  section's decision links gathered in one line at its end; dated accounts
  dropped (they live in decisions and releases). Length follows the content:
  long is not by itself the fault, but it is a smell, and shorter is better
  where it says the same. The paragraph the operator pasted, §First steps
  step 4's 761 words on publishing a target order, is the first passage
  rewritten. Rejected: splitting the guide into several files now (axis:
  scope; the operators' links, the generated page and the suites read
  `docs/KIT.md` by path and heading, and a restructure inside one file keeps
  them).
- Nothing is lost: a script under `docs/evidence/WO-281/` lists, for the
  guide at the base and at the candidate, every code span, command-block
  line, link target and heading, and the order's decisions record each item
  the rewrite removes or renames with its reason. Headings that other
  documents, the generated page or the suites name keep their anchors, or
  every reader of them changes in the same order.
- The rules stay as they are; what was missing is anything that holds a
  write-back to them. The final reviewer's reading of each changed standing
  document, which today returns a dated account to repair, also returns a
  passage its reader cannot follow, naming it; one sentence in product 07
  and in the final reviewer's step says so. Rejected: new wording for the
  write-back and `quality pass:` rules (axis: mechanism; the existing rules
  already ask for it and were not applied, and WO-276's record of
  criticisms finds a rule alone the weakest remedy, several classes having
  recurred after a rule against them).
- The document check gains an advisory that lists a standing document's
  paragraphs longer than a figure the budgets file sets, by file and line,
  as a smell for the stage at hand to read and, where the passage is hard
  to follow, rewrite; it refuses nothing and counts no total.
  Rejected: a refusal by length (axis: standard; the operator's is whether
  a reader can follow the text, and WO-238-D005 retired byte ceilings).
- Scope: the kit guide and the map, which every instance carries and a
  person opens. The product documents and product 07 are the next wave, laid
  out under Known issues and filed by a later pass when this rewrite has
  been read.
