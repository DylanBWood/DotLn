**Design (scope discipline):**

*How this order runs.* These rules are the queue form's
(product 07 §The queue form), set down here because they bind this
execution. Where the executor's role text says something else of an order
in parts, this text is the order's.

1. The parts run in the order given. After a part the executor
   regenerates the installed harness where the part changed what it
   emits, and the next part is written against the files as that part
   left them.
2. A part ends when its steps' cases pass, each run alone as a single
   case; the suites that hold what it changed pass, at most three, found
   by the part's last step, a search of the tests and fixtures for every
   wording, pin and list the part changed; what it regenerates is clean
   (`npm run sync` where it edits a generated source);
   `node scripts/docs-check.mjs` and `node scripts/terms.mjs branch` pass
   over its write-backs; its two readings are judged; `npm run resume --
   checkpoint` has made its checkpoint; and
   `docs/evidence/WO-287/standing.md` is rewritten. No part has a ladder
   of its own, and no other suite is run for it: a part that had to be
   perfect before the next began would pay each absorbed order's cycle
   again inside this one, which is what WO-276's frame first asked for
   and the operator replaced within four hours.
3. Two readers go out at each part's end, and the next part begins
   without waiting for either: one combined reviewer, carrying the three
   briefs, over that part's change, on `astra`; and one reader over
   everything changed so far, the three kinds in turn, on the instance's
   `default` worker. Their findings are judged by the round-one standard
   and fixed as they come. No reviewer runs on a clock: WO-276 ran 104
   review rounds, most of them on clocks, and 89 findings still reached
   its final reviews.
4. The critic of conduct goes out every 75 minutes of a dispatch, from
   one interval after the dispatch begins until its passing complete run,
   on `astra`, under the fixed charter and told nothing by the executor.
   Its report goes to the operator whole before it is answered, and each
   point is judged in the decisions with its class. This is the
   operator's default for the form in this repository.
5. At the end the whole change is read once by the paired round (the
   three kinds on the `default` worker and the combined worker on
   `astra`), its findings are judged and fixed, its rechecks follow the
   ladder's admission rule, and then the handoff sequence's one complete
   run is made. A failed complete run's suites are repaired and
   reproduced alone before the next.
6. The standing file says where the work is and nothing else: the parts
   done with their checkpoints, the part and step in hand, what comes
   next, what is undecided, and the gate row or event it was written
   after. It carries no forecast. It is rewritten at each part's end and
   whenever a session stops, and a session may end at a part's boundary.
7. A defect met beside a part's change is fixed in this order. What
   cannot be settled is boarded as a follow-up and does not hold the
   order. A part's write-backs touch only its own sections, and the end
   reads every changed standing section whole before the paired round.
8. At the start the executor states once, in one message, the bound this
   order needs, a worker cap of forty uses and no wall-clock bound, and records the operator's acceptance; it does
   not ask again part by part. Progress is reported as parts done and
   parts left.
9. The verifier reads with one worker for each part and one for what the
   parts share, and then sends workers over whatever files those left
   unread; the final reviewer reads the same way. WO-276's first
   verification, with one reader, found two defects, its second, with
   twenty, eleven more, and its first final review nine more.
10. A part can be cut on the operator's word without rewriting another:
    its criteria carry its letter and its design is its own file. Cutting
    one is an amendment of this order and the removal of the umbrella
    label from its record, which makes that record an open order again.
